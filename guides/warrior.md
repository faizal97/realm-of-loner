A Warrior fights up close with a weapon and takes the hits for the group. In the group finder a Warrior always joins as the tank. You wear mail (or leather and cloth), use a sword, axe or mace, and fight with rage.

### At a glance
- Good at: holding several enemies. [[Thunder Clap]] hits all within 8 m with 2.5 times the threat.
- Good at: staying up. [[Shield Block]], [[Shield Wall]] and [[Last Stand]] skip the global cooldown.
- Good at: gear upgrades. Most attacks are your weapon plus a bonus, so a better weapon raises almost everything.
- Watch out for: rage starts low. Your first seconds in a fight are slow if you come in with none.
- Watch out for: you have no heal you can repeat. [[Last Stand]] (level 54) is on a 5 min cooldown.

### Leveling
- **Rage** goes from 0 to 100. Hitting gives three times as much as being hit for the same damage.
- Rage left after a fight drains 2 per sec. Pull the next enemy soon and you keep the rest.

Alone, in this order:
1. [[Charge]] in: 12 rage and a 1 sec stun. It only works at the start of a fight, on an enemy you haven't hit yet.
2. [[Rend]] on an enemy that will live 9 sec.
3. [[Overpower]] (level 2) whenever it glows: weapon plus 152 for 5 rage.
4. [[Grievous Strike]] (level 36) when it is ready: weapon plus 205, 6 sec cooldown.
5. [[Heroic Strike]] with spare rage.

- **Overpower** lights for 5 sec when your weapon swing is dodged or misses, or when you dodge. At most once every 8 sec.
- **Battle Fury** (level 34): [[Heroic Strike]] and [[Cleave]] can make your next [[Slam]] instant and free. Tap it while it glows.
- Two or more enemies: [[Thunder Clap]], [[Cleave]], [[Whirlwind]] (level 28) and [[Bladestorm]] (level 56) hit everything within 8 m.
- [[Bloodrage]] (level 16) gives 20 rage. [[Berserker Rage]] (level 38) gives 15 rage and 20% faster attacks for 10 sec.
- [[Recklessness]] (level 48): +240 attack power and 15% faster attacks for 15 sec. Save it for a hard fight.
- In trouble, [[Hamstring]] (level 12) slows one enemy's attacks by 50% for 15 sec.
- [[Intimidating Shout]] (level 24) makes everything within 8 m cower for 4 sec, not bosses.
- [[Retaliation]] (level 18) hits back each melee attack for 15 sec, up to 12 times.

### Before every fight
- [[Battle Shout]]: +140 attack power for your party for 2 min. It costs 10 rage, so cast it in a fight or just after one.

### Talents
You get your first point at level 10 and 51 by level 60. The trees hold 51 points together (Arms 18, Fury 17, Protection 16), so at 60 you can have every Warrior talent; you only choose the order. A tree's second row opens at 5 points in it, its last talent at 10. The first reset is free, then 1g, 5g, 10g.

Questing alone, take Fury first: **Cruelty** (+5% crit), **Unbridled Wrath** (10% more physical damage) and **Enrage** (9% faster attacks) all raise your damage. **Death Wish** adds **Blood Frenzy**: your crits can make the next [[Heroic Strike]] cost no rage.

## Tank
### Quick start
- [[Thunder Clap]] on groups, [[Shield Slam]] (level 52) when ready.
- [[Taunt]] anything that turns on a teammate.
- [[Shield Block]] and [[Shield Wall]] before your health drops.

### Stats
- **Armor** cuts physical hits, not spells.
- **Stamina**: 10 health per point.
- **Agility**: 1% dodge and 1% crit per 20.
- **Strength**: 2 attack power per point. It adds to every weapon hit, more on a slow weapon.

### How to play it
As the tank, your damage makes 1.9 times the threat.
1. [[Thunder Clap]] with two or more enemies: all within 8 m, 2.5 times the threat.
2. [[Shield Slam]]: weapon plus 230, double threat.
3. [[Overpower]] when it glows.
4. [[Heroic Strike]] with spare rage: 1.5 times threat.

- [[Taunt]] (level 10): match the top threat on an enemy; it attacks you for 3 sec. 10 sec cooldown.
- [[Intercept]] (level 44): leap up to 25 m and stun for 3 sec, not bosses.
- **Unyielding** (Protection talent): a dodge can make [[Shield Slam]] and [[Shield Block]] ready and free.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Shield Block]] (level 20): +40% dodge for 5 sec. 10 sec cooldown.
- [[Shield Wall]] (level 32): absorbs 1,380 damage for 10 sec. 3 min cooldown.
- [[Last Stand]] (level 54): heals you for about 1,500. 5 min cooldown.
- [[Rallying Cry]] (level 58): +120 Stamina for the party for 10 sec. 3 min cooldown.
- Only [[Rallying Cry]] uses the global cooldown.

### Talents
Protection first: it holds your threat and survival, and all 16 points fit by level 25. **Defiance** adds 15% threat, **Toughness** 10% armor, **Iron Will** 10% Stamina. The **Last Stand** talent (not the ability) cuts damage taken by 8% and turns on **Unyielding**. **Deflection** (Arms) adds 5% dodge for more Unyielding.
