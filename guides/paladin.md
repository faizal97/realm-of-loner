A Paladin fights in melee with holy spells on top, and in a group fills two roles: healer or tank. You choose the role each time you join a group; your gear stays the same. Paladins wear mail, use a mace, sword or axe, and run on mana.

### At a glance
- Good at: emergencies. [[Divine Protection]] blocks all damage and [[Lay on Hands]] is a big free heal.
- Good at: party buffs. Two free auras and three blessings that all stack.
- Watch out for: mana. Almost everything costs it, your Oaths included.
- Watch out for: no taunt. As a tank you hold enemies with damage only.

### Leveling
- [[Oath of Righteousness]] adds Holy damage to every weapon hit for 30 sec. It only works in combat.
- [[Verdict]] (level 2) needs an Oath and ends it. Cast the Oath again right after.
- **Fervor**: your weapon hits can make [[Verdict]] ready again. It needs an Oath, so keep one up.
- [[Exorcism]] (level 16), [[Hammer of Wrath]] (level 26, 1 sec cast) and [[Holy Shock]] (level 32) are your big Holy hits.
- **Holy Momentum** (level 26): [[Verdict]] can make [[Exorcism]] ready and free. Tap it while it glows.
- [[Oath of Command]] (level 34) hits harder than [[Oath of Righteousness]] for about the same mana. One Oath at a time.
- [[Radiant Wrath]] (level 38): +150 attack power and +100 spell power for 20 sec, 3 min cooldown.
- [[Zealot Strike]] (level 48): weapon damage plus a bonus, 6 sec cooldown.
- Against groups: [[Consecration]] (level 20) and [[Holy Wrath]] (level 28) hit everything around you.
- [[Hammer of the Righteous]] (level 54) and [[Divine Storm]] (level 56) cost a flat 60 and 80 mana: cheap at 60.
- Spells with a cast time stop your weapon swings while you cast.
- Mana only comes back once 5 sec have passed since your last spell that cost mana.

### Before every fight
- [[Steadfast Aura]] on your party: armor for 30 min, free.
- [[Thorned Aura]] (level 18): enemies that hit your party in melee take damage, for 30 min or 60 hits. Free, and it runs with [[Steadfast Aura]].
- [[Blessing of Might]] (level 12): attack power for 5 min.
- [[Blessing of Wisdom]] (level 24): Spirit for 10 min.
- [[Blessing of Kings]] (level 36): all stats for 10 min. All three blessings stack.

### Talents
You get your first point at level 10 and 51 by level 60. Holy and Retribution hold 18 points each, Protection 16, so you can fill two trees and all but one point of the third. A tree's second row opens at 5 points in it, the third at 10. The first reset is free; then 1g, 5g, 10g.

Retribution suits leveling. **Vengeance** raises your Holy and physical damage and turns on **Vengeful Strike**: your crits can make [[Zealot Strike]] ready ([[Exorcism]] before level 48). **Benediction** and **Improved Oath of Righteousness** help [[Oath of Righteousness]] only, not [[Oath of Command]].

## Healer
### Quick start
- Heal with [[Holy Light]]. Start the cast before the tank is low.
- Watch your mana: about 1,800 at level 60 with no gear, five [[Holy Light]] casts.
- Save [[Lay on Hands]] for someone about to die.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to [[Holy Light]] and your Holy damage spells, not to [[Lay on Hands]].
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point, and 1% spell crit per 60. A crit heals 50% more; both your heals can crit.
- **Spirit** only refills mana once 5 sec have passed since your last spell that cost mana. It helps most between pulls.

### How to play it
- [[Holy Light]]: 2.5 sec cast, about 525 healing for 330 mana.
- Each hit you take while casting pushes the cast back, twice at most.
- Heal the damage that is there, not what might come: mana is your limit.
- [[Radiant Wrath]] adds 100 spell power for 20 sec. Use it for the hardest part of a fight.
- **Radiance** (with **Divine Favor**): [[Holy Light]] can make [[Holy Shock]] ready and free. Holy Shock deals damage; it doesn't heal.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Lay on Hands]]: 2,200 healing on anyone, 10 min cooldown.
- [[Divine Protection]]: no damage for 6 sec when an enemy turns on you. 5 min cooldown, combat only.
- [[Hammer of Order]] (stun 3 sec) or [[Repentance]] (level 44, 6 sec) stops an enemy on you. Not on bosses.

### Talents
Holy first: all 18 points fit. **Healing Light** makes [[Holy Light]] heal 20% more and **Illumination** makes it 15% cheaper. **Improved Lay on Hands** takes 4 min off its cooldown. **Divine Favor** adds 5% spell crit and 5% healing. In Protection, **Redoubt** cuts the damage you take.

## Tank
### Quick start
- Open with [[Consecration]] when there is more than one enemy: double threat on all of them.
- Keep [[Holy Shield]] (level 52) up: it absorbs damage for 10 sec on a 10 sec cooldown.
- You have no taunt. If an enemy leaves you, stun it with [[Hammer of Order]] and hit it.

### Stats
- **Armor** cuts physical damage. [[Steadfast Aura]] adds 515.
- **Stamina**: 10 health per point.
- **Agility**: 1% dodge and 1% crit per 20.
- **Strength**: 2 attack power per point.
- **Spell power** adds to your Holy damage, and so to your threat, and to [[Holy Shield]].

### How to play it
- As a tank, all your damage makes 90% more threat. Enemies attack whoever has the most.
- [[Consecration]] (8 sec cooldown) and [[Holy Wrath]] make double threat.
- [[Hammer of the Righteous]] (level 54) and [[Verdict]] make 50% more threat. Recast your Oath after [[Verdict]].
- [[Holy Shield]] absorbs 1,340 damage. It doesn't use the global cooldown.
- [[Holy Shield]] and a priest's [[Word of Warding]] share one shield: the one cast last replaces the other.
- **Bulwark** (with **Blessing of Sanctuary**): when you dodge, [[Holy Shield]] ([[Consecration]] before level 52) can be ready and free.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Divine Protection]]: no damage for 6 sec, and enemies stay on you.
- [[Aura Mastery]] (level 58): +1,700 armor for the party for 10 sec, 2 min cooldown.
- [[Lay on Hands]]: 2,200 healing on yourself or a teammate.

### Talents
Protection first: all 16 points fit. **Improved Steadfast Aura** adds 25% to the aura's armor, **Toughness** raises your armor, **Redoubt** cuts damage taken. **Guardian's Favor** shortens [[Hammer of Order]] and [[Divine Protection]]. **Blessing of Sanctuary** adds 20% threat and 3% less damage taken, and turns on **Bulwark**.
