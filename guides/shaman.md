A Shaman fights with spells and totems from range and can heal or deal damage; you choose the role each time you join a group. Shamans wear leather and use a mace, axe, staff or dagger. Your resource is mana.

### At a glance
- Good at: steady spell damage for little mana, thanks to [[Searing Totem]].
- Good at: big single-target heals and party totems.
- Watch out for: your close-range totems reach only 8 m around you, and in a group you stand back.

### Leveling
- Your auto attack is a shot from range with your weapon's damage. You never need to stand in melee for it.
- [[Searing Totem]] (level 10) and [[Flame Shock]] (level 12) burn the enemy while you cast [[Lightning Bolt]].
- [[Frost Shock]] (level 16) slows an enemy's attacks and movement by 50% for 8 sec. Use it when a fight turns bad.
- Solo, most enemies fight you up close, so [[Fire Nova Totem]] (level 24) and [[Magma Totem]] (level 32) reach them.
- Heal yourself with [[Healing Wave]] between fights. Spirit refills mana only 5 sec after your last spell.

### Before every fight
- [[Lightning Shield]] (level 8): three charges hit enemies who strike you in melee. Lasts 10 min or until used.
- A weapon imbue, [[Earthen Weapon]] first, adds damage to every hit with your weapon, your shot from range included, for 5 min. One imbue at a time.
- In a group fight, drop [[Stoneskin Totem]] (armor) and [[Strength of Earth Totem]] (Strength) for the party, 2 min each.

### Talents
You get your first point at level 10 and 51 by level 60. Elemental holds 18 points, Enhancement 19 and Restoration 21, so you can fill two trees and part of the third. The first reset is free, then 1g, 5g and 10g.

## Healer
### Quick start
- [[Chain Heal]] (level 34) on whoever is hurt most: it heals the most for its mana.
- [[Lesser Healing Wave]] (level 20) when someone is about to die.
- Watch for **Tidal Surge**: tap [[Chain Heal]] while it glows.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to every heal. [[Chain Heal]] and [[Healing Wave]] gain twice as much from it as [[Lesser Healing Wave]].
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point, and 1% spell crit per 60. All your heals can crit, for 50% more.
- **Spirit** refills mana only once 5 sec have passed since your last spell, so it helps most between pulls.

### How to play it
Numbers at level 60 with no gear and no talents:
- [[Chain Heal]] heals about 1.9 health per mana. Despite its name it heals one target. A 2.5 sec cast.
- [[Healing Wave]] heals about 1.6 health per mana, also 2.5 sec.
- [[Lesser Healing Wave]] heals about 1.4 health per mana in 1.5 sec.
- **Tidal Surge** (with the **Healing Way** talent): [[Healing Wave]] and [[Lesser Healing Wave]] can make your next [[Chain Heal]] instant and free.
- [[Stoneskin Totem]] at the start of each pull: the armor helps your tank most.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Earth Shield]] (level 44) absorbs damage on you only, not on the tank. It doesn't use the global cooldown; 30 sec cooldown.
- [[Bloodlust]] (level 58): your party casts and attacks 30% faster for 20 sec. 5 min cooldown.
- [[Wellspring Totem]] (level 38) adds Spirit to the party for 12 sec. It counts as a spell, so it gives mana only if you then stop casting for 5 sec.

### Talents
Restoration first: all 21 points fit. **Healing Way** turns on Tidal Surge. Then Elemental for **Call of Thunder**: spell crit makes heals crit too.

## Damage
### Quick start
- Keep [[Searing Totem]] and [[Flame Shock]] burning on your target.
- Fill with [[Lightning Bolt]].
- Tap [[Earth Shock]] and [[Chain Lightning]] when they glow: they are free.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to every spell. [[Lightning Bolt]] gains the most from it.
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point, and 1% spell crit per 60.
- **Strength** (2 attack power per point) and **Agility** (1% crit per 20) add to your auto attack, not to your spells.

### How to play it
1. [[Searing Totem]] on any enemy that will live a while: 30 sec of burning, by far your most damage for its mana. Recast early and you lose the ticks it had left.
2. [[Flame Shock]] when its burn has ended.
3. [[Earth Shock]] and [[Chain Lightning]] when they glow.
4. [[Lightning Bolt]] to fill the gaps.

Reactions light up your spells:
- **Stormcall**: a [[Lightning Bolt]] hit can make [[Earth Shock]] ready and free.
- **Thunderhead** (level 26): a [[Lightning Bolt]] hit can make [[Chain Lightning]] instant, free and ready.
- **Molten Fury** (with **Elemental Fury**): a [[Lightning Bolt]] or [[Chain Lightning]] crit makes [[Earthquake]] ready and free ([[Flame Shock]] before level 52).

Against groups, [[Chain Lightning]] and [[Earthquake]] (level 52) land around your target, so they work from the back line. [[Elemental Mastery]] (level 48) adds spell power for 15 sec in a fight.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Frost Shock]] halves an enemy's attack speed for 8 sec.
- [[Thunderstorm]] (level 56) stuns enemies within 8 m of you for 2 sec, but not bosses.
- [[Earth Shield]] (level 44) absorbs damage on you, then [[Lesser Healing Wave]] on yourself.

### Talents
Elemental first: all 18 points fit, and **Elemental Fury** turns on Molten Fury. Then Enhancement: **Flurry** makes you attack and cast 8% faster.
