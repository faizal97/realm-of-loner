A Priest heals or deals Shadow damage, and chooses the role each time it joins a group. On your own you fight with Holy and Shadow spells and heal yourself between hits. Priests wear cloth, use a mace or a staff, and cast with mana.

### At a glance
- Good at: keeping one target alive with big, efficient heals and shields.
- Good at: steady solo damage from [[Word of Pain]] for little mana.
- Watch out for: cloth armor. Enemies that reach you hit hard.
- Watch out for: mana. Spirit only refills it once you stop spending for 5 sec.

### Leveling
Alone, you deal the damage and heal yourself. Your damage, in order:
1. [[Word of Pain]] (level 2) first: instant, and the most damage for its mana. Recast it only when it ends.
2. [[Holy Fire]] (level 20), a 3 sec cast that also burns for 10 sec. [[Penance]] (level 56) when ready: instant.
3. [[Smite]] or [[Mind Flay]] (level 26) to fill. Mind Flay also slows the enemy by 50% for 3 sec (a quarter of that on a boss).

[[Mind Blast]] (level 4) casts in 1.5 sec but does the least damage for its mana of your single-target spells.

Your spells light up. Tap them while they glow:
- **Dawnlight**: a [[Smite]] that hits can make your next Smite or [[Lesser Heal]] instant.
- **Dark Thoughts** (level 26): while [[Word of Pain]] hurts an enemy, [[Mind Blast]] can become ready and instant.
- **Creeping Dark** (with the **Shadowform** talent): while [[Word of Pain]] hurts an enemy, your next [[Mind Flay]] can cost no mana.

To stay alive:
- [[Word of Warding]] (level 6) on yourself when an enemy reaches you. You can't get another one for 15 sec.
- [[Psychic Scream]] (level 18): enemies within 8 m flee in fear for 3 sec. Not bosses.
- [[Fade]] (level 24) clears your threat. Alone, the enemies on you also stand still for 2.5 sec.
- [[Desperate Prayer]] (level 38) heals you at once for no mana, 3 min cooldown.
- [[Vampiric Embrace]] (level 52) heals you over 20 sec for 316 mana, 20 sec cooldown.

### Before every fight
- [[Word of Fortitude]]: Stamina for 30 min. Cast in a fight, it reaches your group within 40 m; outside a fight only you get it.
- [[Prayer of Fortitude]] (level 48): more Stamina (86 against 51) for 1 hour. The one you cast last replaces the other.
- [[Inner Fire]] (level 14): 750 armor and some attack power for 10 min, on yourself.
- [[Shadowform]] (level 36): 73 spell power and 150 armor for 30 min, on yourself. Casting a heal ends it; [[Vampiric Embrace]] and shields don't.

### Talents
Your first point comes at level 10, and you have 51 at level 60. The trees hold 17 (Discipline), 19 (Holy) and 16 (Shadow) points, 52 in all, so at 60 you can take everything but one point. The order only matters while you level. The first reset is free; after that it costs 1g, then 5g, then 10g.

The **Surge of Power** and **Shadowform** talents are passives. The [[Surge of Power]] and [[Shadowform]] spells come with your level either way.

## Healer
### Quick start
- Keep [[Renew]] on the tank and use [[Heal]] as your main heal.
- [[Greater Heal]] for heavy hits; [[Flash Heal]] when a heal has to land fast.
- Watch your mana: it only refills after 5 sec without spending any.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to heals and shields, most to [[Greater Heal]].
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point, and 1% spell crit per 60. Only direct heals crit.
- **Spirit** refills mana 5 sec after your last spell that cost mana.

### How to play it
Health per mana at level 60, no gear:
- [[Heal]] (level 16): about 2.3, a 3 sec cast.
- [[Greater Heal]] (level 34): about 2.1, 3 sec, your biggest heal.
- [[Renew]]: about 1.9, instant, over 15 sec.
- [[Lesser Heal]]: about 1.8, 2 sec.
- [[Flash Heal]] (level 28): about 1.7, your fastest cast at 1.5 sec.

Tap a lit spell while it glows:
- **Answered Prayer** (**Inspiration**): [[Flash Heal]] and [[Heal]] can make your next [[Greater Heal]] or Heal instant.
- **Steady Grace** (the **Surge of Power** talent): your heals can make [[Penance]] ready.

Save [[Surge of Power]] (level 44) for the hardest part of a fight: spell power and 15% faster casting for 15 sec.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Pain Suppression]] (level 54): one target absorbs 1780 damage for 8 sec. 2 min cooldown.
- [[Divine Hymn]] (level 58): heals one target 3360 over 8 sec. 3 min cooldown.
- Enemies on you: [[Fade]] clears your threat; [[Psychic Scream]] scares them off for 3 sec.
- You are low: [[Desperate Prayer]], an instant free heal.

### Talents
Holy first, all 19 points: **Spiritual Healing** and **Inspiration** raise your healing, **Divine Fury** speeds up [[Heal]] and [[Lesser Heal]] by 0.5 sec. Then Discipline: **Mental Agility** makes every spell 10% cheaper.

## Damage
### Quick start
- [[Shadowform]] (level 36) before the fight, then [[Word of Pain]] on your target.
- [[Mind Blast]] when **Dark Thoughts** lights it; [[Mind Flay]] (level 26) to fill.
- Too much threat: [[Fade]] clears it.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to every spell.
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point, and 1% spell crit per 60.
- **Spirit** refills mana 5 sec after your last spell that cost mana.

### How to play it
1. [[Word of Pain]]: the most damage for its mana. Recast it only when it ends.
2. [[Holy Fire]] (level 20): a 3 sec cast that also burns for 10 sec.
3. [[Penance]] (level 56) when ready: instant.
4. [[Mind Blast]] when it glows: ready and instant.
5. [[Mind Flay]] to fill. With **Creeping Dark** lit it costs no mana.

- [[Shadowform]]: 73 spell power and 150 armor for 30 min, on yourself.
- [[Vampiric Embrace]] (level 52) heals you over 20 sec.
- **Creeping Dark** needs the **Shadowform** talent.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Fade]] (level 24): enemies lose all threat on you. 30 sec cooldown.
- [[Word of Warding]] on yourself when an enemy reaches you.
- [[Psychic Scream]]: enemies within 8 m flee for 3 sec. Not bosses.
- [[Desperate Prayer]] (level 38): an instant heal for no mana.

### Talents
Shadow first: **Shadow Focus**, **Darkness** and the **Shadowform** talent each raise your Shadow damage, 26% together, and the Shadowform talent also cuts damage taken by 5%. **Improved Word of Pain** adds 30% to Word of Pain. Then Discipline: **Mental Agility** makes every spell 10% cheaper.
