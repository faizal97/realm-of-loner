A Warlock deals damage, mostly with curses and burns that keep hurting while you cast the next spell, and fights with a demon at its side. It fills one role: damage. You wear cloth, use a staff, dagger or sword, and spend mana, which [[Life Tap]] refills from your health.

### At a glance
- Good at: damage for little mana, from spells that hurt over time.
- Good at: fighting alone, with a Voidwalker that pulls enemies off you.
- Watch out for: low health in cloth, and casts pushed back when you are hit.

### Leveling
- You start with an **Imp**, [[Shadow Bolt]] and [[Immolate]]. [[Corruption]] comes at level 2, [[Curse of Agony]] at level 8.
- [[Life Tap]] (level 6) turns health into mana. Health refills between fights too: tap when it is full and mana isn't.
- At level 10, summon a **Voidwalker**: every 5 sec it pulls a creature that is hitting you onto itself.
- [[Shadow Ward]] (level 14) and [[Death Coil]] (level 24) keep you alive when a fight goes badly.
- [[Siphon Life]] (level 32) is one more spell over time; [[Conflagrate]] (level 34) an instant hit.

### Before every fight
- [[Demon Skin]] raises your armor for 30 min. From level 18, [[Demon Armor]] gives more armor, adds Stamina and replaces Demon Skin.
- Have a demon out. Summoning takes 6 sec, works only out of a fight and costs 25% of your max mana (Imp) or 40% (Voidwalker).
- You have one demon at a time. It attacks your target, comes into dungeons and heals between fights. If it dies, summon it again.
- The **Imp** throws Firebolt every 2.2 sec but has little health. The **Voidwalker** has much more health and armor.

### Talents
You get your first point at level 10 and 51 by level 60. The trees hold 17, 15 and 19 points, 51 in all, so at level 60 you have every Warlock talent: the order only matters while you level. The first reset is free, then 1g, 5g and 10g.

## Damage
### Quick start
- Put [[Curse of Agony]], [[Corruption]] and [[Immolate]] on the target, then fill with [[Shadow Bolt]].
- Recast each when it wears off: an early recast loses its last ticks.
- Tap a spell while it glows: it is instant, ready or free.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to every spell, except the haunting damage of [[Haunt]].
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point, and 1% spell crit per 60.
- **Stamina**: 10 health per point, so more [[Life Tap]]s.
- **Spirit** refills mana 5 sec after you last spent some.

### How to play it
1. Spells over time, which stack: [[Siphon Life]] (level 32) does about 3.8 damage per mana, [[Shadow Bolt]] 0.8.
2. [[Curse of Doom]] (level 36) on a boss: 1680 after 20 sec.
3. [[Haunt]] (level 56) every 8 sec, then [[Conflagrate]] and [[Shadowburn]] (level 20).
4. [[Incinerate]] (level 52) to fill: twice the damage per second of [[Shadow Bolt]].

- **Dark Tide**: [[Corruption]] and [[Immolate]] ticks can make [[Shadow Bolt]] instant.
- **Kindled Ruin** (level 34): [[Immolate]] ticks can make [[Conflagrate]] ready and free.
- **Withering** (**Amplify Curse**): [[Curse of Agony]] ticks can make [[Haunt]] ready and instant.
- **Soulfire Rush** (**Soul Link**): [[Shadow Bolt]] can make [[Soul Fire]] ready and instant.
- **Ruinous Flame** (**Ruin**): a [[Shadow Bolt]] or [[Incinerate]] crit readies a free [[Shadowburn]].
- Boss cooldowns: [[Demonic Sacrifice]] (level 54) and [[Metamorphosis]] (level 58), 30 sec of spell power.
- Groups: [[Shadow Bolt Volley]] (level 48) and [[Shadowfury]] (level 44). Leave out [[Searing Pain]]: it draws threat.

### When it goes wrong
- [[Shadow Ward]] absorbs 540 damage of any kind. Cast it in a fight.
- [[Death Coil]] stuns one enemy for 3 sec. [[Howl of Terror]] (level 26) makes all within 8 m flee for 4 sec.
- Neither works on a boss.

### Talents
Affliction first: it raises your spells over time and turns on **Withering**. Then Destruction (**Devastation**, **Ruin**), then Demonology.
