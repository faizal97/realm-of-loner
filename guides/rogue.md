A Rogue fights in melee: quick strikes build combo points, then a finisher spends them. You can only fill the damage role. Rogues wear leather and use one dagger or sword, and run on energy: 100 at most, full at the start of every fight, refilling 10 per second.

### At a glance
- Good at: steady single-target damage, and bleeds on enemies that live a while.
- Good at: stopping enemies with [[Gouge]], [[Blind]] and [[Kidney Shot]], and getting out of trouble.
- Watch out for: energy, not time, sets your pace. Stuns don't work on bosses.

### Leveling
- From level 1: [[Sinister Strike]] builds combo points, [[Eviscerate]] spends them.
- [[Gouge]] (level 3) stops an enemy for 4 sec, and your damage doesn't break it. Use it on a second enemy while you kill the first.
- [[Evasion]] (level 8): 50% more dodge for 15 sec when a fight goes badly.
- [[Slice and Dice]] (level 10) and [[Rupture]] (level 16) are your first finishers for longer fights.
- [[Ambush]] (level 20) opens a fight with a big hit and 2 combo points.
- [[Feint]] (level 28): alone, the enemies attacking you stop for 2.5 sec. 10 sec cooldown.
- [[Blind]] (level 32) stops an enemy for 8 sec, a second enemy out of the fight.

### Talents
You get your first point at level 10 and 51 by level 60. Assassination holds 17 points, Combat 18 and Subtlety 16: 51 in all, so at level 60 you have every talent. The order only matters while you level. The first reset is free; then 1g, 5g, 10g.

## Damage
### Quick start
- Build to 5 combo points with [[Sinister Strike]], then [[Eviscerate]]: it glows at 5.
- Keep [[Slice and Dice]] up, and [[Rupture]] on enemies that live 10 sec or more.
- Tap a glowing ability: it is free or ready.

### Stats
- **Agility**: 1 attack power per point, and 1% crit and 1% dodge per 20.
- **Strength**: 1 attack power per point.
- **Attack power**: 1 damage per 14 on each weapon hit, per second of weapon speed.
- **Haste** speeds up auto attacks only.

### How to play it
Combo points belong to one enemy: switching starts again from 0.

1. [[Eviscerate]] at 5 combo points.
2. [[Slice and Dice]]: 20% faster attacks, 9 sec plus 3 per point.
3. [[Rupture]] at 5 combo points on an enemy that will live 10 sec: each point adds a fifth of the bleed. Don't recast it early.
4. Build with [[Ghostly Strike]] (level 38) when ready, else [[Hemorrhage]] (level 48): less energy than [[Sinister Strike]], more damage.

Open with [[Ambush]] or [[Cheap Shot]] (level 26): within 3 sec of reaching melee, one per enemy.

Reactions:
- **Blade Rush** (level 24): [[Sinister Strike]] can make [[Eviscerate]] free.
- **Cold Edge** (**Relentless Strikes**): [[Eviscerate]] can make [[Sinister Strike]] free.
- **Blade Dance** (the **Blade Flurry** talent): weapon hits can make [[Killing Spree]] ready.
- **Quiet Blade** (**Preparation**): a dodge can make [[Backstab]] free. Otherwise skip it.

Stack [[Cold Blood]] (level 54) and [[Shadow Dance]] (level 58) with [[Killing Spree]] (level 56).

### When it goes wrong
- [[Evasion]]: 50% more dodge for 15 sec. No help against spells.
- [[Cloak of Shadows]] (level 44): no damage at all for 5 sec.
- [[Feint]] before you pull an enemy off the tank.
- Stuns fail on bosses; [[Kidney Shot]] still spends your points.

### Talents
Combat first: **Weapon Expertise** raises physical damage, which is all your damage. Then Assassination, then Subtlety.
