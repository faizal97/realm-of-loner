A Bard heals or deals damage with songs that keep playing while you do other things, and chooses which each time it joins a group. Bards wear leather, use a dagger or staff, carry an instrument in the ranged slot, and run on mana.

### At a glance
- Good at: healing the whole party at once, a little at a time.
- Good at: party buffs, and a song that makes the whole party attack and cast faster.
- Watch out for: songs reward rhythm. Tap on the beat and they grow; tap early or let them run out and they shrink.
- Watch out for: a song tap is a spell, so mana only refills once 5 sec have passed since your last tap.

### How songs work
- A song plays for one round (12 sec on the Lute) and pulses every 3 sec. Starting it costs its full mana.
- The beat is the last part of the round (2 sec on the Lute). Tap the song on the beat for a verse and another round. Each verse makes it 20% stronger, up to 3.
- Tap early and you lose a verse. Let it run out and it stops, with every verse lost.
- At full verses, tap off the beat for the Finale: every verse at once in one burst. The song plays on from no verses.
- At full verses, a tap on the beat keeps the song at full.
- A tap on a song already playing costs a quarter of starting it, and has a 0.5 sec global cooldown.
- Two songs play at once; a third replaces the one you started first. A second song joins the first one's beat, so you tap them together.
- A song still playing when a fight ends carries into the next fight, verses and all.

### Instruments
Pick one outside a fight with its button on your bar. The item in your ranged slot gives stats only. Every instrument costs the same mana per second while you keep your songs going.
- [[Play the Lute]] (level 1): 12 sec rounds, a 2 sec beat, 3 verses.
- [[Play the Drum]] (level 10): fast. 8 sec rounds, a 1.5 sec beat, 3 verses. Damage songs hit 15% softer, healing songs 10% softer, Finales 20% softer.
- [[Play the Horn]] (level 20): slow and forgiving. 16 sec rounds, a 3 sec beat, 2 verses. Damage songs hit 15% harder, healing songs 25% softer, Finales 60% harder.

Played well, the three end up within 5% of each other for damage and for healing. Pick the rhythm you land best.

### Leveling
- At level 4 a letter starts the Rehearsal: a practice fight against a target that never strikes back. You can stop it at any time.
- Open with [[Dirge]] (level 4) and keep it on the beat; cast [[Dissonant Note]] between beats.
- Keep [[Song of Rest]] (level 4) playing when a fight runs long. It heals you too.
- [[Sharp Refrain]] (level 8) is instant, on a 6 sec cooldown.
- [[Lullaby]] (level 10) puts nearby enemies (not bosses) to sleep for 3 sec, when more than you can handle come at once.
- From level 16, [[Ballad of Blades]] hits your target and every enemy near it.
- [[Sour Note]] (level 6) cuts off an enemy's cast: free, 12 sec cooldown, no global cooldown. From level 10, casters out in the world cast a spell behind a red bar every 14 sec; missed, it hits twice as hard.
- **Pull** on the Fight list brings a pack of 2 (3 from creature level 20) at once, each with half the health, damage and XP.

### Before every fight
- [[Hearthsong]] (level 26) on your party: 30 Spirit and 22 Intellect for 30 min at level 60.
- [[Anthem of Stone]] (level 40): 250 armor and 17 Stamina for 30 min at level 60. It doesn't replace Hearthsong, so cast both.
- Choose your instrument. You can't change it in a fight.

### Talents
Your first point comes at level 10 and you have 51 by level 60. A tree's second row opens at 5 points in it, the third at 10. Each tree's third row holds two talents of one point, and you can learn only one of them. That leaves 17 points in Battle Hymn, 17 in Harmony and 15 in Tempo, 49 in all, so at 60 you have every talent apart from the keystones you didn't choose. The order only matters while you level. The first reset is free; then 1g, 5g and 10g.

## Healer
### Quick start
- Keep [[Song of Rest]] playing and tap it on the beat.
- At full verses, tap it off the beat for its Finale when the party is hurt.
- [[Counterpoint]] and [[Verse of Mending]] on the tank.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to every heal. [[Crescendo]] gains the most per cast; songs gain a little on every pulse, for everyone.
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point and 1% spell crit per 60. Only direct heals crit ([[Soothing Chord]], [[Crescendo]], [[Chorus of the Grove]], [[Standing Ovation]]). Songs and heals over time never do.
- **Spirit** refills mana only once 5 sec have passed since your last spell or song tap. At level 60 with no gear that is about 22 mana a second.

### How to play it
At level 60 with no gear, on the Lute:
- [[Song of Rest]] heals everyone in your party for about 10 every 3 sec, 15 at 3 verses. A round at full verses heals a party of five for 304, for a 70 mana tap: about 4 health per mana. Starting it costs 281.
- Its Finale at 3 verses heals everyone for 137 at once.
- [[Chorus of the Grove]] (level 18) heals everyone for about 131, under 2 health per mana in a party of five. 15 sec cooldown.
- [[Counterpoint]] (level 22) absorbs 520 damage on one friend for 15 sec. 6 sec cooldown.
- [[Verse of Mending]] (level 14) heals one friend for 429 over 15 sec.
- [[Crescendo]] (level 30) heals one friend for about 722, a 3 sec cast. [[Soothing Chord]] heals for about 377 in 1.8 sec.

When only the tank is hurt, a healing song mostly heals people who don't need it. Keep the tank up with [[Counterpoint]], [[Verse of Mending]] and [[Soothing Chord]], and save the song's mana.

Keep [[Marching Song]] (level 6) as your second song: your party attacks and casts about 8% faster, 12.5% at 3 verses. Its Finale gives three times that for 6 sec.
- A **red bar** over a boss names its attack and its answer: [[Step Back]] for an attack on the group, [[Sour Note]] for a heal or a spell. Missed, an attack hits 2.5 times as hard and a heal heals 1.5 times (more on a Hard raid).
- A red bar on a slam at the tank: [[Verse of Mending]] (level 14) or [[Counterpoint]] (level 22) on the tank before it lands makes it smaller, and so does each interrupt.

### When it goes wrong
- A Finale is your fastest party heal. Keep a song at full verses for the moment the party needs it.
- [[Standing Ovation]] (level 60): heals everyone for about 321 at once. 2 min cooldown.
- [[Encore]] (level 50): heals everyone for about 295 over 12 sec. 3 min cooldown.
- [[Intermission]] (level 20): 30% of your mana back over 8 sec while you are not casting. 3 min cooldown, no global cooldown.
- [[Cleansing Verse]] (level 12) removes every poison, curse and other damage over time an enemy put on a friend.

### Talents
Harmony first: all 17 points fit. **Soothing Voice** raises [[Song of Rest]], **Resonance** gives mana back on every beat, **Rising Finale** makes healing Finales bigger the more hurt the party is, and **Heartstrings** heals your most hurt ally on every beat. Then choose **Rescue Note** (a healing Finale ready at any verse when an ally drops under 25%) or **Sustain** (a song about to run out holds on 3 sec, all of it on the beat).

Then Tempo: **Practised Hands** widens the beat, **Fourth Verse** adds a verse, and **Second Wind** gives the party mana, rage or energy on [[Marching Song]]'s Finale. Its keystone is **Medley** (three songs at once) or **Perfect Pitch** (a beat landed in its last 0.5 sec adds two verses).

## Damage
### Quick start
- Keep [[Dirge]] on your target and tap it on the beat.
- [[Ballad of Blades]] as your second song when more than one enemy is close together.
- Cast [[Sharp Refrain]] and [[Shatterchord]] between beats.

### Stats
- **Spell power** adds to every song and spell.
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point and 1% spell crit per 60. Songs and their Finales never crit.
- **Spirit** refills mana only once 5 sec have passed since your last spell or song tap.

### How to play it
At level 60 with no gear, on the Lute:
1. [[Mocking Verse]] (level 44) on the target: your whole party deals 8% more damage to it for 15 sec.
2. [[Dirge]]: 45 Shadow damage every 3 sec, 71 at 3 verses. Its Finale at 3 verses deals 321.
3. [[Ballad of Blades]]: 31 Arcane damage every 3 sec to the target and every enemy near it, 50 at 3 verses.
4. Between beats: [[Sharp Refrain]] (about 216, instant, 6 sec cooldown), then [[Shatterchord]] (about 568, a 2.5 sec cast) on an enemy that will live through it.

Watch the beat while you cast: a 2.5 sec cast started just before the beat runs past it.

Against groups: [[Ballad of Blades]] and [[Requiem]] (level 56, about 245 to every enemy near you, 10 sec cooldown).
- A **red bar** over a boss names its attack and its answer: [[Step Back]] for an attack on the group, [[Sour Note]] for a heal or a spell. Missed, an attack hits 2.5 times as hard and a heal heals 1.5 times (more on a Hard raid).

### When it goes wrong
- [[Stage Fright]] (level 36): one enemy (not a boss) runs from you for 6 sec. 30 sec cooldown.
- [[Lullaby]]: nearby enemies (not bosses) sleep for 3 sec.
- [[Echo]] (level 52) plays your last Finale again at half strength. 2 min cooldown.
- [[Song of Rest]] heals you when you are alone and hurt.

### Talents
Battle Hymn first: all 17 points fit. **Rousing Verses** raises your damage songs and their Finales, **Taut Strings** your Arcane damage, **Lingering Dirge** leaves a Dirge on enemies a damage Finale hits, and **Drumbeat** strikes the target on every beat. Then choose **Showstopper** (after a damage Finale your next [[Shatterchord]] is instant) or **Syncopation** (a beat on one song adds a verse to your other songs, once every 10 sec).

Then Tempo for **Practised Hands** and **Fourth Verse**, or Harmony for **Resonance**'s mana.
