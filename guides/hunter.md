A Hunter fights from range with a bow and a tamed beast at its side. It fills one role: damage. Hunters wear leather, use an axe, sword or dagger in melee, and run on mana.

### At a glance
- Good at: playing alone. Your pet pulls monsters off you while you shoot.
- Good at: steady damage. Auto shot costs no mana and fires all fight.
- Watch out for: mana. Your big shots cost a lot, so a long fight drains you.
- Watch out for: bosses. Your stuns don't work on them.

### Leveling
- [[Arcane Shot]] (level 2) and [[Venom Sting]] (level 4) are your first damage. Keep [[Venom Sting]] up on anything that lives 15 sec.
- At level 10, tame a pet: a normal beast (not elite, not named) at or below your level. It takes 6 sec.
- Every beast fights the same, at your level. Pick the one you like.
- Your pet keeps its health between fights and heals quickly out of combat. If it dies, tap it to revive it out of combat: 6 sec and 30% of your mana.
- [[Concussive Shot]] (level 8) slows an enemy by 50% for 4 sec.
- [[Immolation Trap]] (level 18) and [[Spirit of the Hawk]] (level 20) raise your damage.
- [[Feign Death]] (level 24) saves a pull that goes wrong.
- [[Aimed Shot]] (level 28) is your big hit until [[Kill Command]] (level 52) and [[Twinfang Shot]] (level 56).

### Before every fight
- [[Spirit of the Hawk]] (level 20): attack power, 80 at level 60, for 30 min.
- [[Spirit of the Monkey]] (level 4): 8% more dodge for 30 min. It doesn't replace [[Spirit of the Hawk]]; you can have both.
- [[Keen Eye Aura]] (level 34): attack power for your whole party, 90 at level 60. It costs no mana.
- Check your pet is alive: it joins every fight, dungeons included.

### Talents
You get your first talent point at level 10 and 51 by level 60. Beast Mastery holds 19 points, Marksmanship 21 and Survival 14, so you can't fill all three. The first reset is free; after that it costs 1g, then 5g, then 10g.

## Damage
### Quick start
- Keep [[Hunter's Mark]], [[Venom Sting]] and [[Immolation Trap]] up on anything that lives a while.
- Fire [[Twinfang Shot]], [[Kill Command]] and [[Aimed Shot]] when they are ready.
- Tap [[Arcane Shot]] when it glows: it is free then.

### Stats
- **Agility**: 2 ranged attack power per point, and 1% crit and 1% dodge per 20.
- **Your bow** sets auto shot damage. A slower bow hits harder per shot.
- **Intellect**: 15 mana per point. Shots that aren't physical ([[Arcane Shot]], [[Twinfang Shot]], [[Volley]], [[Explosive Trap]]) crit 1% per 60.
- **Spirit** refills mana once 5 sec pass without spending any. Auto shots are free.
- **Strength** only helps your melee attacks.

### How to play it
1. [[Hunter's Mark]]: 10% more damage from you and your pet.
2. [[Venom Sting]] and [[Immolation Trap]]: the most damage for their mana. Don't recast early.
3. [[Twinfang Shot]], [[Kill Command]], then [[Aimed Shot]] (2.5 sec cast).
4. [[Arcane Shot]] when it glows.

- **Swift Quiver**: an auto shot can make [[Arcane Shot]] ready and free.
- **Steady Aim** (level 28): [[Arcane Shot]] can make [[Aimed Shot]] instant.
- Burst: [[Rapid Fire]], [[Bestial Wrath]] and [[Rapid Killing]] stack.
- Groups: [[Multi-Shot]], [[Volley]] and [[Explosive Trap]].

### When it goes wrong
- [[Feign Death]] drops all your threat. With your pet out, enemies turn to it.
- [[Scatter Shot]] stuns for 4 sec, [[Sleep Sting]] for 6 sec. Not on bosses.
- [[Deterrence]]: 60% more dodge for 10 sec, against physical attacks.

### Talents
Marksmanship first: all 21 points fit. The **Keen Eye Aura** talent turns on **Dead Aim**: an [[Arcane Shot]] or [[Aimed Shot]] crit makes [[Twinfang Shot]] ready.
- Beast Mastery: a stronger pet, and **Pack Call** lights [[Kill Command]].
- Survival: you take less damage, and **Quick Reflexes** lights [[Counterattack]] when you dodge.
