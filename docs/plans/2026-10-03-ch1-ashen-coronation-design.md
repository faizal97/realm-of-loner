# The Ruinfall Saga, Chapter 1: The Ashen Coronation (design draft)

v11.0 and v11.1. **Approved by Faizal on 2026-10-03 and parked** ("yes approve for chapter 1. we will not work on
that until i asked to", saga decision 19): no issues, milestones or art until he asks. The saga's decisions are in
`2026-10-03-ossarak-saga.md` (numbers below refer to them). Drafted by the game designer, 2026-10-03.

## What Chapter 1 is

Veshmira is dead, and not one debt died with her. Her eldest, Kethriax (whom the player already knows as Lord Kethran
Vale), opens Cinderpeak Spire, takes up the Ledger's books and calls everything due on **Tamlin's coronation day**.
The Accord has to rely on the woman who sold it (Thorne) to read the books; the Krugar's High Chief steps out for the
first time to answer that the Krugar pay in steel (Grask). In the Spire raid (v11.1) the player learns what the debt
really was: not gold but blood, **the oldest debt**, owed by every black dragon to Ossarak, whose blood they hatched
from (decision 15). Kethriax's death pays the last of it, and the wound in the sky opens on the Unmoored Reach.

- **Level 60 only.** Everyone who plays it is already 60, and progression after 60 is horizontal (decision 16).
- **The existing world, changed** (decision 5): Kingsmere, Vazhrak, Rumhook Bay and Cinderpeak get new areas, quests
  and story; no new region.
- **The Accord and the Krugar stay enemies** (decision 4): separate staging, a clash on Cinderpeak.

## v11.0 stands on its own

The gap to v11.1 is **about two weeks as a soft target, not a commitment** (Faizal: "yes probably but dont too rely on
it"). So nothing in v11.0 depends on the raid shipping by a set date:
- **The story is complete up to the raid's door.** v11.0 ends with a closing scene: the books are read, the coronation
  survived, the Krugar have their notes back, and Kethriax has sealed himself at the top of the Spire. "He's waiting
  for us." An ending with a door in it, not a cliffhanger mid-scene.
- **The rewards make sense with or without the raid.** The dungeon blues are the best blues in the game but stay under
  today's ceiling (about 97% of the Tidecrown Citadel), so nothing outgrows current content before the raid. When the
  raid raises the ceiling, they settle at the blue cap of the new one. One week or four, they're worth the runs.
- **No dates in the game or the notes:** the zone says the Spire's top is sealed, never "coming soon"; the What's new
  notes don't promise a date.
- **The dungeons join the Trials rotation and pay Marks** like every level-60 dungeon, so they keep their value after
  the raid too.

## v11.0: the story, the zone, the two dungeons

### The zone: Cinderpeak, the Spire awake (level 60)

The existing Cinderpeak (51–60) gains a level-60 area on the Spire's flanks, reached from each faction's existing
camp nearby. About **six new places**, like each Drowned Crown zone: the Spire's Gate, the Ash Terraces, the Counting
Galleries (the Ledger's working floors cut into the rock), the Whelping Pens, the Burnt Archive, and each faction's
forward camp. Enemies: the Brood's dragonkin, whelps, the Ledger's human enforcers, and fire-bound constructs. It gets
a **level-60 music variant** and its own art pack. Pre-v11 Cinderpeak stays as it is for levelling players.

### The Accord's story (Kingsmere → the Gaol → Cinderpeak → the coronation)

1. **The notices.** Kingsmere is dressed for the coronation. Notices on every door: every debt in the kingdom falls due
   on Coronation Day. Regent Carrow and Marshal Hale know the books are in the Spire and that nobody can read them.
2. **The bargain (Thorne).** In Kingsmere Gaol, Thorne is the only one who can read Kethriax's books. She asks for
   nothing but one thing: to be there when the books are closed. "I kept the accounts. I would see them balanced."
3. **The Counting Galleries.** With Thorne's notes, the player works through the Spire's flanks (zone quests).
   Clash beat: the books show that the Krugar war chest paid for Accord walls, and a Krugar strike has burned pages
   the Accord needed.
4. **The coronation (dungeon: the Accord's).** Tamlin's beat: **he finishes the oath while the hall shakes** (one
   line, decision 8).

### The Krugar's story (Vazhrak → Rumhook Bay → Cinderpeak)

1. **The war council (Grask).** Grask appears in person for the first time. Kethriax has called in the war chest the
   Krugar borrowed against. Tarro of Ossa speaks for Greensward ("whoever harms the land answers to every hornfolk")
   and is overruled (decision 8). Grask: **"The Krugar pay in steel."**
2. **The counting-house (dungeon: the Krugar's).** The Krugar's notes are held by the Saltpenny trading house in
   Rumhook Bay. They take them back.
3. **The other flank.** A Krugar forward camp on Cinderpeak's other side; strikes on the Ledger's caravans. Clash
   beat: the burned pages the Accord needed.

### The two dungeons (5 players, level 60, about 4 bosses each, one per faction as in the Drowned Crown)

- **Accord: "The Ashen Court"** (working name). Kingsmere Keep on Coronation Day: the Brood's agents and the last of
  Marrow's cabal strike during the oath. The cabal's names come out of the books here (saga thread 5); its last
  member is the final boss.
- **Krugar: "The Saltpenny Vault"** (working name). Under the Saltpenny house in Rumhook Bay, where the Ledger's
  southern notes are kept: the house's bruisers, its strongroom's guardian, and the Ledger's southern factor (one of
  the Brood) at the bottom.
- Each has a first-entry lore intro (the rule for every dungeon), joins the Trials rotation later, and drops one
  effect item from its final boss (the v10.10 rule).

### Rewards: gearing up for the raid (decision 16)

The gap between v11.0 and v11.1 is for playing the story and gearing up, not levelling. So:
- **The dungeons' blues** drop at the **blue cap of the raid's coming ceiling**: today's ceiling is the Tidecrown
  Citadel (100%), and the Spire raid raises it about 5% (the horizontal-progression rule), so the blues land at about
  92% of the new ceiling (about 97% of today's). They're the best blues in the game, and stay worth having after the
  raid opens.
- **Mentor Marks** from both dungeons and the zone's quests, as level-60 dungeons pay today, so older gear can be
  upgraded toward the ceiling too.
- **The bar for the raid:** a group in v11.0 dungeon gear can clear the Spire raid's first bosses on Normal (the
  Balance Analyst measures it, below). That number is a check on the soft two-week gap: the time a typical player takes to
  reach that gear.
- No new currency: Marks and drops, as everything after 60 already uses.

### The Legends (filler, the game designer's part)

- **Widya, a Songbook chapter:** in the Counting Galleries she finds Reedsong's note among the Ledger's pages. She
  burns it and writes the song of the day the hamlet stopped owing (saga thread 4, closed). Small, about her want.
- **Bromli, a cameo at the coronation feast:** he's there for a ballad ("A coronation AND a dragon? Write fast.").
  One scene, and he joins as a cameo in group runs as now.
- **Lyveus guards the coronation** (decision 18): in the Accord's dungeon he confronts the cabal's last member himself,
  and his name is cleared before King Tamlin, closing his cabal arc. He stays free for later chapters.

## v11.1: the Spire raid and the teaser

### "The Spire of Kethriax" (working name; 10 players, Normal and Hard, about 5 bosses)

Both factions stage separately (the Accord from its Cinderpeak camp, the Krugar from theirs), as for Veshmira's Lair.
A sketch of the order:
1. **The Gatewarden:** a dragonkin captain at the Spire's gate.
2. **The Clerks of the Brood:** a council of drakonid scribes who write the debts into the walls.
3. **The Hoard-Bound:** a construct of the Ledger's gold and ash.
4. **Thorne's last scene:** not a boss. She reads the last page of the books and finds it isn't a sum of gold but a
   debt of blood: the Brood's to Ossarak. She closes the book. "I kept the accounts. I never knew whose they were."
5. **Kethriax:** first as Lord Kethran Vale, then in his true form. He says he isn't collecting for himself; he's
   paying.

### The teaser cinematic (decision 9; in-game only)

Kethriax dies, and with his last breath the oldest debt is paid. Over Kinloch, the old scar Silverleaf was founded to
watch tears open in the sky, and behind it a broken land hangs over nothing: the Unmoored Reach. Starfeather sees it
from Silverleaf; far away in Redmarch, Zul'kesh sees a second wound open. **The longest cinematic yet**, with its own
music.

## Canon, logo, music

- **canon.md:** the Timeline (the Brood hatched from Ossarak's blood; the Ledger as Veshmira's payment), the Names
  (the Unmoored Reach, the oldest debt, every new character and place), and the Reveals: **level 60** for the blood
  origin, the Ledger serving Ossarak, and the Unmoored Reach. Characters and places new to v11 are named only at 60.
  The lorekeeper checks it all.
- **Logo and emblem (v11.0):** the **Ruinfall Saga emblem** (decision 17), drawn once and carried on every chapter's
  logo, the promo videos and the devlogs, and **"The Ashen Coronation" logo** that carries it (decision 13). Both drawn
  in the art pipeline with one contact sheet for Faizal's review, shown on the chapter's loading screen and in What's
  new.
- **Music** (ships only once Faizal's ears approve it): Cinderpeak at 60, the two dungeons, the coronation, the raid
  (a three-part theme like the other raids), and the teaser cinematic.

## The proof (Balance Analyst)

- **The dungeons:** wipes per run and par times like every dungeon (#28/#39 rules: normal pace beats par 20–30%,
  fast is a trade).
- **The gear gap:** from a fresh 60 in level-60 dungeon blues, the number of v11.0 dungeon runs and hours (at an hour a
  day) to reach the raid's entry bar. That's the input to Faizal's gap decision.
- **The raid:** Normal and Hard, as the existing raids are checked (wipes, Hard's extra mechanic per boss).
- **Effect items** from the new finals pass the effects bars (the v10.10 rules).

## Size, from this team's throughput

- **v11.0:** about 5–6 betas, roughly 25–30 h of developer work (zone, quests, two dungeons, cutscenes, two art
  packs), plus about 3–4 h of sim CPU.
- **v11.1:** about 3 betas, roughly 12–15 h (the raid, the cinematic), plus about 2–3 h of sim CPU.
- **The real limits:** Faizal's reviews of the art contact sheets, the cutscenes and the music, and the gap between
  the releases.

## Candidates for Chapter 1 (from player feedback)

- **One original ability per class** (#142): learned at 60 through a short Chapter 1 quest, built for this game's systems (item effects, Omens, the party's bots), a sidegrade or a new decision, never more power. Designed when Chapter 1 resumes.
