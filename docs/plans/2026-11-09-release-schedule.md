# Release schedule to the new year (decided 2026-11-09)

## How we ship

- **Saturday:** one grand feature (medium to big), plus every improvement checked on `/dev/` by Thursday's freeze.
  Each grand feature leaves players something to do for the next one or two weeks, not only a system.
- **Weekdays:** patches, web only, no ping, bug fixes only, shipped as soon as they are ready.
- A grand feature that isn't ready moves to the next Saturday and the next one takes its slot. Two orders are fixed:
  Dispelling before Chapter 1 (its bosses use it), and the raid after Chapter 1.
- Version numbers never show in the game, so the minor numbers below may shift without anything breaking.

No week is empty: the pre-patch runs into its finale, Chapter 1 into Longnight, Longnight into the raid.
