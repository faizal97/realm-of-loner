# The Ruinfall Saga opening movie

A 73.6-second movie set to the saga's theme song: it introduces the saga, says it has three chapters, flashes the three
chapter logos with a glimpse of each, then says "but first" and lands on the pre-patch, The Notes Come Due. No narrator;
the pictures tell it, with a few short title lines.

## In the game

- `src/opening.js` adds the chapter `ruinfall_opening` (`movie: true`, level 60, music `ruinfall_opening`). Its shots are
  built on first use from a timeline that follows the song; every shot is cut into pieces of at most 2 seconds, each a
  new framing of the same picture, except the two end cards.
- It plays **once per account**: the first time a character who has reached the pre-patch (level 60 and a raid cleared,
  `G.prepatchOpen()`) enters the world while `D.PREPATCH.live`. Seen chapters live in the account-wide cutscene store
  (`azsolo.story`), so a second character never sees it again. It replays from the Theater ("Opening movie").
- A movie in `CS.play`: picture only (no caption bar or title), letterboxed and graded (`.cs.movie`), taps do not skip
  shots (only Skip ends it), and shots are timed from the movie's start so a late frame never drifts off the beat.
- The song (`audio/out/music_ruinfall_opening.m4a`, the saga theme trimmed to 73.6 s) ships as a music file. The movie
  waits up to 6 s for it to decode (`SND.preload`), then starts it with the first shot.
- Art: `src/art_ruinfall_opening.js` (`ART.opening(key)`), generated by `art/promo/trailers/pack.py` from the eight movie
  backdrops and figures in `art/promo/trailers/art/`, the chapter logos (`art/ruinfall/chapters.py`) and the pre-patch
  logo (`art/ruinfall/prepatch_logo.py`).

## As a video

`art/promo/export_trailer.sh opening <out.mp4> ruinfall_opening` records the same cut from a build (with "Coming soon"
on the last card) at 1920x1080, 30 fps. `art/promo/trailers/ruinfall.js` is the older 66-second trailer, kept.

## Spoiler rule

Shown: the story up to the pre-patch (levels 1-60, Veshmira, the storm, the debt notes). Not shown: anything of Chapter
One beyond its setting (the vault in Cinderpeak Spire, the coronation in Kingsmere), the new keeper, Kethriax, Ossarak's
tie to the Ledger. Chapters Two and Three appear by name only.
