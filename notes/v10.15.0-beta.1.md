Test build, browser only: play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Boss attacks now warn first: a red bar over the boss, and one answer that saves you.
- Every class gets an interrupt, and slams on the tank are the whole group's to answer.
- Dungeons and raids are tuned so playing well matters: careless groups wipe more.

**New Content**
- Added an interrupt for every class, free, from level 6: **Hilt Bash**, **Throat Jab**, **Spellbreak**, **Hush**, **Storm Snap**, **Choke Hex**, **Pinning Shot**, **Stern Word**, **Thornlash** and **Sour Note**.
- Added red bars: **Searing Breath**, **Whirling Blades**, **Molten Splash** and **Shadow Lance** warn first, and **Step Back** avoids them.
- Added team slams: a shield or quick heal on the tank, or an interrupt on the boss, makes the slam smaller; the tank's defensive avoids it.
- Added each boss's own slam, such as **Skagg Smash**, **Belly Flop**, **Magma Fists** and **Tidal Crush**.

**Changes**
- Raid slams can no longer be fully blocked: the tank's defensive makes them smaller instead.
- A **Bard**'s song still playing when a fight ends now carries into the next fight, verses and all.
- A healer **Bard** now switches to **Song of Rest** once the group is hurt.

**Balance**
- **The Smugglers' Deep** and **The Dreaming Caves**: damage ×0.75. **Kingsmere Gaol** ×0.8, **Gearhollow** ×0.9, **The Gemfall Caves** and **Pyre Abbey: Cathedral** ×0.95.
- **The Blackcloister**, **Graymouth** and **Temple of Shal'zua**: damage ×1.4; **The Sunken Archive** ×1.65.
- **The Magma Throne** and **The Tidecrown Citadel** (Normal): damage ×1.7.
- **Veshmira** (Normal) now breathes fire over the whole raid every 25 sec.
- *Dev note: good groups at level 60 never wiped, and some dungeons below 60 wiped good groups half the time. Both now sit where playing well is what decides it.*

**Interface**
- Added a speech bubble over whoever speaks in a fight, so boss lines and group callouts no longer hide in the chat.
- The answer's button now glows while a red bar fills, and the boss card names each attack and its answer.
- A slam's bar now shows its size shrinking as your group answers it, with a mark for each answer.
- The death recap now says what was missed, such as "no Step Back".
- The fight header now keeps room for your name, with buffs folded into one tap that opens the full list.
- **Leave group** now shows on the scene after a dungeon or raid is cleared.
- The new talent point line now opens **Talents** when tapped.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed your name disappearing in fights when buffs showed.
- Fixed **Leave group** sitting under the action bar after a raid.
- Fixed simulated players sometimes getting names from another world.
- Fixed a **Black Dragonspawn** line giving away a later story reveal.

**Known Issues**
- Red-bar and slam numbers are still being tuned and will change between betas.
- Open-world elites and casters don't warn yet.
