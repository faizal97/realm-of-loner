Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**A tap that can't act now says why**
- A greyed button gives its reason on the button itself, in a dashed outline so it reads as "not now" rather than broken: "Queued", "In queue for <activity>", "Bags full", "Need 2g 40s" (the exact shortfall), "Learn Riding first", "You're home".
- The action bar dims while you travel or are dead, and a tap says why ("You're travelling." / "You're dead. You revive in 60 sec."). Cooldowns keep ticking on it.
- Tapping a gathering node in a fight says "Finish the fight first."
- At a vendor, items the vendor won't buy dim, and a tap says "The vendor won't buy <item>."
- A bounty you've taken opens its details (where to hunt, progress).
- "Not ready" and "Busy" are gone. You now see "Still casting <spell>" or "<spell> isn't ready (12s)". A tap during the short global cooldown stays quiet.

**You see what really happened**
- **Selling:** a "Last sale" line in the vendor's header shows what you sold and for how much, and your money flashes gold. Green items or better, and anything marked as an upgrade, ask first ("Sell <item>?" with **Keep** and **Sell**). Greys and whites still sell in one tap.
- **Create all** that stops because your bags filled says so: "Bags full. Made 3 of 10." A refused cook no longer shows "Perfect!" or "Burnt" on top of the refusal.
- **Loot rolls:** the timer bar goes grey and says "Paused while you fight" or "Paused while you look" while it waits. If it runs out, the Loot log says "Time ran out: you passed on <item>." (with a notice if it was an upgrade).
- **Queue:** "Queued 3m" now shows in your frame during a fight too, and tapping it opens your queue.
- **Quest items you pick up** (not kill drops): the quest objective says where, for example "Collect them here" or "Collect them in Halden Vineyards" (or "Dropped by <creature>" for a drop). Tap "Collect them here" to go to the Collect button.

**Chat requests**
- An open request shows the time it has left at the end of the line ("4m", then "<1m"). When it runs out it stays in chat, greyed, and reads "expired".
- Help and guild applications close only if they worked. A refusal says why and leaves the request open. A group that already found someone shows "Someone else took this spot".

**Heal target and ready checks**
- The Party header now reads **"Healing: you"** or **"Healing: <name>"** (in their class colour), with "tap a member to heal them" when nobody is picked.
- "Ready ✓" is green and clearly done, not greyed out like a broken button. The ✓ marks and the "Waiting for <name>" line have their own look, and the line turns green when everyone is ready.
- When the member you were healing dies or leaves, you get a yellow notice rather than a red error.

**Talents at level 60**
- Rogue, Warrior and Mage earned more talent points than their trees could hold: 7, 3 and 3 points could never be spent. Each now has new talents, so every class can spend all 51.
- Rogue: Quick Finish (Assassination), Precise Strikes (Combat) and Shadowed Step (Subtlety). Warrior: Heavy Swings (Arms). Mage: Biting Cold (Frost).
- Your saved talents stay as they are, and the points you couldn't spend before are free to use.
- The effects Stubborn Blood and Steady Fuse were retuned alongside the new talents, so their items give up a little more or less of their stats.

**PvP at normal speed**
- Every PvP fight (duels, the Bloodsand Brawl, battlegrounds and War Mode ambushes) now runs at normal speed, whatever your battle speed is set to, and the speed chip is hidden in all of them. Your setting comes back in the next fight against monsters.
- The first PvP fight of a session tells you: "PvP fights run at normal speed."

**After a wipe or a death**
- After a wipe in a dungeon or raid, and after you die in the open world, a recap lists who died and what killed them (the last hit and its size). Under each name: how much damage they took in the last 5 seconds, how long before the death their last heal landed, and the healer's state at that moment (for example out of mana or dead). It only states facts, so you can see what happened.
- Tap **Deaths** between pulls to open it again. If you died yourself, it opens straight away.

**Quest experience**
- Quest XP now falls once you are well past a quest: full XP up to 2 levels over it, then 20% less for each level beyond that, down to 20%. Kills already worked this way. The quest log shows it before you take the quest: "(reduced: you're 4 levels over)".
- A few quests that sat in the wrong zone, 10 levels or more above it, were moved to where they belong.

**Gear before level 60**
- A blue is now clearly better than a green of its level (about 20% more), and generated greens and blues roll their stats a little higher or lower (up to 10% either way), so a good find and a poor one both exist. Gear stats no longer dip at level 60.
- Dungeon blues are never weaker than a random blue of their level.
- Quest rewards for gear offer **two versions** with different stat mixes in the same armour type; tap **Choose** on the one you want (it then reads "Chosen ✓"). Weapons can be any type your class uses, bows included.
- From level 30, a dungeon's last boss has a 2% chance and a rare creature 5% of dropping a level-appropriate epic that fits your class. Expect one to three in a whole levelling journey. Level-60 gear is unchanged.
- Armour names now say what they are made of: Stitched Leather (was Handstitched), Silk (was Soft) and Linked (was Rusted). Items you already own keep their names.

**Balance**
- Creatures below level 20 bring a friend into a fight half as often (7% of pulls, was 14%), and the friend arrives after 4–7 seconds instead of 2–5. A creature that is going to join shows in the fight list straight away, marked "joins in 4 s", while you quest too, not only in dungeons. Solo Mages were dying in these fights around level 15: they now die much less. Some fights at levels 15 to 25 are still too hard for Mages and Priests, and that continues next beta.
