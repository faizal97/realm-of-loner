Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**A tap that can't act now says why**
- A greyed button now gives its reason on the button itself, in a dashed outline so it reads as "not now" rather than broken: "Queued", "In queue for The Smoke Pit", "Bags full", "Need 2g 40s", "Learn Riding first", "You're home".
- The action bar dims while you travel or are dead, and a tap says why ("You're travelling." / "You're dead. Release or wait to revive."). Cooldowns keep ticking on it.
- Tapping a gathering node in a fight says "Finish the fight first."
- At a vendor, items the vendor won't buy dim, and a tap says "The vendor won't buy <item>."
- A bounty you've taken opens its details (where to hunt, progress). One you've already handed in says when new ones come.
- "Not ready" and "Busy" are gone. You now see "Still casting <spell>" or "<spell> isn't ready (12s)".

**You see what really happened**
- **Selling:** a "Last sale" line under the vendor's money shows what you sold and for how much. Green items or better, and anything marked as an upgrade, ask first ("Sell <item>?" with **Keep** and **Sell**). Greys and whites still sell in one tap.
- **Create all** that stops because your bags filled says so: "Bags full. Made 3 of 10." A refused cook no longer shows "Perfect!" or "Burnt" on top of the refusal.
- **Loot rolls:** the timer bar goes grey and says "Paused while you fight" or "Paused while you look" while it waits. If it runs out, the Loot log says "Time ran out: you passed on <item>." (with a toast if it was an upgrade).
- **Queue:** "Queued 3m" now shows in your frame during a fight too, and tapping it opens your queue.
- **Quest items you pick up** (not kill drops) are called "Collect <item>", and the tracker says where to collect them.

**Chat requests**
- An open request shows the time it has left ("4m", then "<1m"). When it runs out it stays in chat, greyed, and reads "expired".
- Help and guild applications close only if they worked. A refusal says why (for example "Leave your current group first.") and leaves the request open. A group that already found someone says "Someone else took this spot".

**Heal target and ready checks**
- "Ready ✓" is now green and clearly done, not greyed out like a broken button.
- The ✓ marks and the "Waiting for <name>" line have their own look: green marks, the waiting member's name in their class colour.
- When the member you were healing dies or leaves, the message is a yellow notice rather than a red error.

**Talents at level 60**
- Rogue, Warrior and Mage earned more talent points than their trees could hold: 7, 3 and 3 points could never be spent. Each now has new talents, so every class can spend every point.
- Rogue: Quick Finish (Assassination), Precise Strikes (Combat) and Shadowed Step (Subtlety). Warrior: Heavy Swings (Arms). Mage: Biting Cold (Frost).
- Your saved talents stay as they are, and the points you couldn't spend before are now free to use.

**PvP at normal speed**
- Every PvP fight (duels, the Bloodsand Brawl, battlegrounds and War Mode ambushes) now runs at normal speed, whatever your battle speed is set to. The speed chip is hidden in all of them. Your setting comes back in the next fight against monsters.
- The first PvP fight of a session tells you: "PvP fights run at normal speed."

**After a wipe or a death**
- After a wipe in a dungeon or raid, and after you die in the open world, a recap lists who died and what killed them (the last hit and its size). Under each name: how much damage they took in the last 5 seconds, how long before the death their last heal landed, and the healer's state at that moment (for example out of mana, dead, or casting on someone else). It only states facts, so you can see what happened.
- Tap **Deaths** between pulls to open it again. If you died yourself, it opens straight away.

**Quest experience**
- Quest XP now falls once you are well past a quest: full XP up to 2 levels over it, then 20% less for each level beyond that, down to 20%. Kills already worked this way.
- The quest log shows it before you take the quest: the XP is greyed and says "reduced: you're 4 levels over".
- A few quests that sat in the wrong zone, 10 levels or more above it, were moved to where they belong.

**Balance**
- Creatures below level 20 bring a friend into a fight half as often (7% of pulls, was 14%), and the friend arrives after 4–7 seconds instead of 2–5. A creature that is going to join shows in the fight list straight away, marked with when it arrives. Solo Mages and Priests were dying in these fights between levels 15 and 25.

**Gear before level 60**
- A blue is now clearly better than a green of its level (about 20% more), and generated greens and blues roll their stats a little higher or lower (up to 10% either way), so a good find and a poor one both exist.
- Dungeon blues are never weaker than a random blue of their level.
- Quest rewards for gear offer two versions with different stats, can be any weapon your class uses (bows included), and can be head, shoulder or neck pieces.
- From level 30, a dungeon's last boss has a 2% chance and a rare creature 5% of dropping a level-appropriate epic that fits your class. Expect one to three in a whole levelling journey. Level-60 gear is unchanged.
