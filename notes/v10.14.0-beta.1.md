Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- New class to try in the beta: the **Bard**, a healer or caster who fights with songs played on the beat.
- Spells now queue: tap the next one just before a cast ends and it starts the moment it can.

**New Content**
- Added the **Bard** for every race (beta only for now), with daggers, staves and an instrument.
- Added songs: **Song of Rest**, **Dirge**, **Marching Song** and **Ballad of Blades** play over time; tap again on the beat for a verse, up to 3.
- Added the **Finale**: at full verses, tap a song off the beat to spend every verse in one burst.
- Added instruments that change the rhythm: **Lute** (level 1), **Drum** (10) and **Horn** (20), chosen outside a fight.
- Added **Sharp Refrain**, **Cleansing Verse**, **Intermission**, **Shatterchord**, **Stage Fright**, **Mocking Verse** and **Echo**.

**Changes**
- A tap in the last 0.4 sec of a cast or global cooldown now waits and fires when it ends, instead of "still casting".
- **Lanternwake** now follows its real dates in the beta too (24 October – 7 November); test festival rewards are removed.

**Interface**
- Ability buttons now sweep until your cast ends, not just the global cooldown.
- Song buttons now show the song's time left, a gold beat zone, a gold edge on the beat and your verses.
- The **Lanternwake** keepsake now reads "Your first Wickless Knight kill this year".

**Known Issues**
- The Bard is not finished: its talents, art and how-to-play guide come in later betas, and its numbers will change.
