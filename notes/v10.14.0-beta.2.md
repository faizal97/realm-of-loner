Test build, browser only: play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- The **Bard** no longer crashes on its first loot.
- Bard songs now have their own sounds and music.

**New Content**
- Added Bard song sounds: a note in your instrument's voice, higher with each verse on the beat, a chord for the **Finale**.
- Added Bard song music: while you sing, the song plays over the fight, one more part with each verse.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed the **Bard** crashing the game when it picked up loot.
- Fixed the **Bard** getting no weapon from the first militia quest; it now gets the **Militia Quarterstaff**.

**Known Issues**
- The **Bard** is not finished: its talents, art and how-to-play guide come in later betas, and its numbers will change.
