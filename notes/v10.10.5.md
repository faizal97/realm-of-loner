Druids can hold Bear Form again.

**Fixes**
- Bear Form no longer drops back to your normal form on its own a few seconds into a fight. It lasts until you tap it again or the fight ends.
- Your rage no longer resets to 0 in the middle of a fight, so you can build enough for Maul and the other bear attacks.
- The cause was the game's automatic save, which ran every few seconds during fights and shifted you out of your form. A save in a fight now leaves your character as it is.
