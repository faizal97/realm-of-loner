Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Your alts now share one realm: the same players on every character.
- Tap a player to see their gear, their guild and the runs you did together.

**Changes**
- Every character on your account now sees the same players. Older saves join the oldest character's realm.
- Friends no longer quit. They go on a break for a few days, whisper you, and come back.
- Up to 80 players are now level 60. The levelling zones are as busy as before.
- New players are the most likely to quit. Guildmates say goodbye in guild chat before they go.
- Players now keep their gear between groups, and get new gear when they level.
- Random armour names now match their level: **Linen** and **Rawhide** up to 19, **Gossamer** and **Wyvernhide** from 40.

**Interface**
- Added the player card. Tap a player in the scene, the guild list or chat, or hold a party frame.
- The card shows their gear, their guild, recent levels and clears, runs with you, ninja rolls, friends and rival.
- Added **Remove friend** to the player card.
- **Invite** for players nearby is now on their player card.
