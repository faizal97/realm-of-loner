Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Added the **Account bank**: one bank all your characters share.
- Bags and the bank got a full overhaul: filter tabs, smaller slots, **Store materials**, **Buy back** and **materials bags**.
- Your pair in **Battlegrounds** now wins far more often; bear tanks get real tank upgrades.

**New Content**
- Added the **Account bank**, a tab on every bank: 12 slots shared by all your characters, plus 3 tabs for 200, 400 and 800 Mentor Marks.
- Added the **Gatherer's Satchel** (12 slots) and **Forager's Haversack** (20 slots), materials bags that hold only trade goods.
- Added both materials bags to **Tailoring** (skill 110 and 255); now and then they're on the auction house too.
- Added 30 dungeon and raid items, levels 23–60, where a class and role had nothing in a slot (caster armor, bows, healing maces, leather belts and boots).
- Added the **Ruinfall Saga** opening movie: it plays once when a character who has reached the pre-patch enters the world, and replays from the **Theater**.

**Changes**
- The auction house now always lists a bag for your level: **Woolen Bag** (8 slots) from 15, **Silk Bag** (10) from 30, **Duskweave Bag** (12) from 45.
- Crafting now uses materials in your bank as well as your bags (the bags' first).
- Vendors now let you **Buy back** your last 12 sales for what they sold for.
- **Sort** now puts upgrades first, and the bank has its own **Sort bank**.
- Added an optional setting to sell grey items when a vendor opens (**Settings → Auto-sell grey items**, off by default).
- Battleground enemies now send one player at your pair, not one or two: games won about 59% → 76%.
- A druid tank's upgrade arrows now weigh tank stats (Strength, Stamina, armor), not Intellect and Spirit.

**Interface**
- Bags and both bank tabs now have filter tabs: **All**, **Gear**, **Materials**, **Use**, with counts.
- Bags now show 7 slots to a row; the ▦ button switches to small slots, 10 to a row.
- The bank is now a grid like the bags, with **Store materials** to move every trade good onto its bank stacks in one tap.
- Tapping an item in your bags now shows a short panel (price, one fact, the buttons); **Inspect** opens the full tooltip.
- A materials bag's free slots are striped green; tap one to see what goes in it.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed an item leaving your bags without reaching the account bank when the device couldn't save.
- Fixed a chat trade taking your payment and dropping the item when your bags had filled since the offer.
- Fixed an old stack over 20 going into the bank as one slot, and **Sort** leaving one over 20.
- Fixed an old quest item in the bank that couldn't be taken out with full bags.
- Fixed **Sell several** selling picked items hidden by a filter tab.
- Fixed the bank showing the upgrade arrow on gear you can't use.
- Fixed the **Events** list wrapping the › onto a second line.
