**Highlights**
- New class: the **Bard**, a healer or caster for every race, who fights with songs played on the beat.
- New **Mail**: a letter for every auction you sell, and expired auctions wait in your mail instead of your bags.
- Spells now queue: tap the next one just before a cast ends and it starts the moment it can.

**New Content**
- Added the **Bard** for every race: heals the whole party with songs or deals Arcane and Shadow damage, with daggers, staves and an instrument.
- Added songs: **Song of Rest**, **Dirge**, **Marching Song** and **Ballad of Blades** play over time; tap again on the beat for a verse.
- Added the **Finale**: at full verses, tap a song off the beat to spend every verse in one burst.
- Added three instruments that set the rhythm: **Lute** (level 1), **Drum** (10) and **Horn** (20), chosen outside a fight.
- Added three Bard talent trees, **Battle Hymn**, **Harmony** and **Tempo**, whose talents change how songs work.
- Added **The Rehearsal**: at level 4 a Bard gets a letter and a practice fight that walks through songs, verses and the Finale.
- Added Bard song sounds and music: each verse on the beat adds a note, and the song plays one more part over the fight.
- Added Bards among the other players: they join your groups as healers and damage.
- Added **Mail**, opened from the envelope beside your gold: a letter for each auction sold, and expired auctions held for you at any town mailbox.
- Added **Edda Marlow**, a general merchant, to the Bank and Auction House in **Kingsmere**.

**Changes**
- A tap in the last 0.4 sec of a cast or global cooldown now waits and fires when it ends, instead of "still casting".
- A sold auction now shows a notice: "Sold: Iron Helm, 2g 40s".
- Players who whisper you to group for a quest or a kill are now within 3 levels of you.

**Interface**
- Added **How Bards play**, a sheet that shows a song on the beat; open it from any song's or instrument's tooltip.
- Added Bard teaching cards the first time something new happens: your first Finale, a song running out, the Drum and the Horn.
- Added the official **Bard** guide to the guide site.
- Song buttons now show the song's time left, a gold beat zone, a gold edge on the beat and your verses.
- Ability buttons now sweep until your cast ends, not just the global cooldown.
- The **Lanternwake** keepsake now reads "Your first Wickless Knight kill this year".

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed the action bar gaining empty slots in a fight, for every class.
- Fixed area stuns and fears such as **Psychic Scream** and **Typhoon** saying you "stomp the ground"; only **War Stomp** does now.

**From earlier patches**
- Fixed **Paws for Proof** getting stuck in the Quest Log for some Accord characters, with no way to turn it in or abandon it.
