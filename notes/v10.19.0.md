**Highlights**
- The Ruinfall Saga pre-patch: **The Notes Come Due**, a new level-60 story, a new ability for every class, and **debt collectors** in every zone.
- New: **Season's Finest**, the best piece for every slot of your class and role, and the **Account bank**, shared by all your characters.
- Group finder players now wear real gear, and dungeons and raids were retuned around them: tougher enemies everywhere, harder raids.

**The Notes Come Due**
- Added **The Notes Come Due**: six quests a faction at 60, after **The Drowned Crown** or any level-60 raid, ending with the title **Reader of Notes**.
- Added **Tutor Ione Calder** in **Rumhook Bay**, who teaches every class its new ability for 6 collectors.
- Added **Intervene** (Warrior): take the next big hit meant for a party member.
- Added **Chronomark** (Mage): set a mark, then snap your health and mana back to it within 8 sec.
- Added **Vow of Atonement** (Priest): for 10 sec, half your spell damage heals your most hurt ally.
- Added **Set Up** (Rogue): a finisher that hands its damage and threat to the next party member to hit.
- Added **Lantern of Faith** (Paladin): for 20 sec, every heal lands half on its target and half on one ally.
- Added **Blood Bargain** (Warlock): for 10 sec, you and your target both take 20% more damage.
- Added **Lure** (Hunter): the next enemy to join the fight is held for 6 sec.
- Added **Seed of Renewal** (Druid): a heal that grows for 10 sec; bloom it early for less, and a big hit crushes it.
- Added **Kinship Totem** (Shaman): for 8 sec, your party's health evens out, the healthy giving to the hurt.
- Added **Duet** (Bard): for 15 sec, your party songs reach one ally only, twice as strong.
- Added **Debt Collectors** (below 30) and **Ledger Collectors** (30 and up) to every levelling zone, dropping **torn debt notes**.
- Added **Clerk Odell Pask** to every trainer town: 40 notes for the title **the Debt-Breaker** (whole account), and at level 60, 10 notes for 10 **Mentor Marks**.
- Added the **Ruinfall Saga** opening movie: it plays once when you reach the pre-patch, and replays from the **Theater**.
- Added a new main menu theme while **The Notes Come Due** is on: **The Coronation Call**.
- Party bots at 60 now know their class's new ability and use it.

**Season's Finest**
- Added **Season's Finest** (Hero → Journey): for each slot of your class and role, a **Best** piece and a **Next best** piece, each Found, Seen or Unknown.
- Added 52 Season's Finest pieces: Best pieces drop in the **Tidecrown Citadel** and in older raids and dungeons, Next best pieces in the older ones.
- Every boss with Season's Finest pieces now rolls for one of them on each kill, on top of its usual loot.
- **Hint** names where an Unknown piece drops; **Reveal** names the item, the boss and the drop chance.
- Find every Best piece of a role before any Hint or Reveal (**Found blind**) to earn **the Tideward** (tank), **the Tidemender** (healer) or **the Tidebreaker** (damage).
- **Pathfinder** counts every class and role you've finished blind, across all your characters.
- Each faction has its own lists, from places your side can enter.
- Season 1, the **Season of the Tides**, starts with this update and runs until the next chapter of the story.

**Gold and Mentor Marks at 60**
- Added **Expert Riding** at level 60 (300g): with a swift mount, roads are 70% faster instead of 40%.
- Added a swift version of each faction's mounts, such as the **Swift Palomino** and **Swift Grey Wolf** (75g each).
- Added 7 **Mentor Quartermaster** mounts for Mentor Marks (500–900), any faction, all swift.
- Added every raid set look in 2 **Quartermaster** colours for the wardrobe (60 Marks an armour look, 70 a weapon look).
- Added 4 armour heirlooms: **Veteran's Jerkin**, **Legguards**, **Handwraps** and **Boots** (60 Marks each).
- Added **Reforge** for level-57+ blue and purple gear: reroll its effect or its stats for Mentor Marks, then Keep or Revert.
- Added **Renown**: an account rank bought with Marks and gold, with a dye each rank, the title **the Renowned** every 5 and a mount tint every 10.
- Added **Crystal Vial** and **Fine Spice**, vendor reagents for level-57+ flasks and **Well Fed** meals (1g 50s each).
- The **Mentor Quartermaster** now has 3 items at 20% off each week.

**Bags and Banks**
- Added the **Account bank**, a tab on every bank: 12 slots shared by all your characters, plus 3 tabs for 200, 400 and 800 Mentor Marks.
- Added 3 bank tabs of 12 slots each (50g, 100g, 200g).
- Added the **Gatherer's Satchel** (12 slots) and **Forager's Haversack** (20 slots), materials bags from **Tailoring** that hold only trade goods.
- Added **Lock**: a locked piece of gear is never sold, thrown away or auctioned. **Lock worn gear** is under **Hero → Equipment**.
- Added **Buy back** at vendors: your last 12 sales, for what they sold for.
- Added **Store materials**, which moves every trade good onto your bank stacks in one tap.
- Added **Settings → Auto-sell grey items** (off by default).
- Crafting now uses materials in your bank as well as your bags.
- **Sort** now puts upgrades first, and the bank has its own **Sort bank**.
- The **Auction House** now always lists a bag for your level, and the basic materials your crafts use at your skill.

**Group Runs and Guilds**
- Added **keybinds**: keys 1–9 fire your action bar. Set your own in **Hero → Abilities → Arrange action bar → Set key**.
- Added tabs to group runs and raids: **Party**, **Next pull** (**Enemies** in a fight) and **Plan**, under the run's name, clock and **Ready** / **Pull**.
- Added raiding guilds, a PvP guild and a Trials guild to each side, and a **Realm ranking** under **Social → Guild**.
- Added **the Crestbearer**: clear the newest raid on **Hard** while it is still the newest.
- Added 30 dungeon and raid items, levels 23–60, where a class and role had nothing in a slot.
- Added new raid music: **Harpsichord Counting** in **Veshmira's Lair**, **Forge of Ender** in **The Magma Throne** and **The Descent of Aelindra** in **The Tidecrown Citadel**.
- Added **Settings → Buzz** (Android): a short buzz on each ability, a double buzz for a red bar.

**Changes**
- Gear now loses 10% durability each time you die (a wipe counts). At 0% it is broken and gives no stats until repaired.
- Repair at any vendor with **Repair all**, or with a **Repair kit** between pulls in a dungeon.
- Group finder players now wear gear in every slot with stats for their role, and healers and casters have spell power.
- A careless player in your group still wears whatever dropped, so a bad draw still costs you something.
- Raids now take players in Rare gear on Normal and Epic gear on Hard.
  *Dev note: a level-60 tank from the group finder had about 2,100 health, against a boss whose ordinary hits land for 1,100–1,800. Now it has about 3,800.*
- Answering a red bar now stops a cast already under way, for you and your group.
- Dungeon reward gear now takes the stats for the role you play.
- Upgrading an item with **Mentor Marks** now raises its damage and armour toward the best raid piece of its kind, never past it. Items you already upgraded are rebuilt once at the power you paid for.
- **Hard** raid drops now go 2 upgrade steps past that, so they are always stronger than the Normal drop.
- **The Daily Board** and **The Weekly Board** now turn at your local midnight and your own Monday.
- The region holding West and East Rotmoor is now called **Rotmoor**.
- On Android, the screen now stays on in fights and group runs.

**Balance: Dungeons**
- Increased enemy health: dungeons below 60 about ×1.7, level-60 dungeons about ×1.9. Damage is unchanged, and par times rose to match.

**Balance: Raids**
- **The Magma Throne**: enemy health ×2, damage ×1.05.
- **The Tidecrown Citadel**: enemy health ×1.7, damage ×1.15.
- **Veshmira's Lair**: enemy health ×1.5, damage ×1.3.
- **Hard** rises by the same amount in each raid, and par times rose to match.
- Raids now pay 15 Mentor Marks on each raid's first clear of the week, then 5 a clear.
  *Dev note: repeating Veshmira's Lair paid more Marks an hour than anything else.*

**Balance: Wanted Elites**
- Increased enemy damage in **Wanted** elites' group runs, more the higher the elite: ×2.2 at level 8, ×3 at 25, ×3.6 from 40.
  *Dev note: once groups wore real gear, no group ever lost to a **Wanted** elite. Trials keep their numbers.*

**Balance: Item Effects**
- Damage: **Opening Cut**'s extra damage at level 60: 124 → 100.
- Damage: **Glass Heart**'s damage bonus: 2.5% → 2.8%.
- Damage: **Steady Fuse** items keep a little less of their stats (cost 56% → 60%).
- Resource: **Tithe of Battle**'s mana: 0.3% → 0.15% per tick, and its items keep a little less of their stats (cost 40% → 45%).
  *Dev note: longer boss fights made it far too strong for warlocks.*
- Survival: **Stubborn Blood** items keep a little less of their stats (cost 56% → 60%).

**Balance: Other**
- Battleground enemies now send one player at your pair, not one or two: games won about 59% → 76%.
- Level-57+ flasks and **Well Fed** meals now need a reagent: keeping both up costs about 3.75g an hour.
  *Dev note: gold piled up at 60 with nothing to spend it on. Levelling crafts take no reagent.*

**Interface**
- Bags and both bank tabs now have filter tabs (**All**, **Gear**, **Materials**, **Use**) and a small-slot view, 10 to a row.
- Tapping an item in your bags now shows a short panel; **Inspect** opens the full tooltip.
- The death recap now shows your gear's condition and the repair cost, and **Hero** shows a badge when your gear is at 50% or less.
- The place name now shows the region too: **Halden Vale · Ambermoor**, the same name the Map uses.
- The guild list now puts the guilds that fit your level first, with each guild's raid progress or Trials rating.
- **Hero → Journey**'s dungeon, Roulette, world boss, featured raid and Trials rows now open the **Groups** tab.
- The **Fight** tab now lists creatures before gathering and fishing.
- Added logos for the **Ruinfall Saga**, the story chapters, the raids, Legends, **Trials**, the **Bloodsand Brawl** and **Honor**, and a crest for every guild.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed spent **Mentor Marks** coming back after you closed the game and moved on the map.
- Fixed account mounts, **Trialsworn** cloaks and festival unlocks not reaching your other devices with cloud save.
- Fixed a heirloom, mount or look bought on two devices before a sync costing Marks twice.
- Fixed some upgrade steps costing **Mentor Marks** without changing the item.
- Fixed a craft losing its materials when the product didn't fit your bags. They now go to the bank.
- Fixed the tank standing behind the group in fights. Allies now keep their order, tank in front.
- Fixed a group leader from chat or **Help Wanted** joining a raid in gear below the raid's level.
- Fixed druid damage players in your groups having half the health of other casters.
- Fixed group players rolling Need on Stamina rings and cloaks when they aren't tanking.
- Fixed a mana potion in **Bear Form** or **Cat Form** filling rage or energy, and the red bar asking a bear for a heal it can't cast.
- Fixed the Android screen dimming and locking during long fights and raid pulls.
- Fixed **Bounty Boards** sending you after monsters you can only reach through the other faction's towns.
- Fixed chat and guild messages that didn't match the game: players who weren't online or in the guild, and talk of fights that never happened.

**From earlier patches**
- At level 60, every town's **Bounty Board** now has bounties for that zone's monsters, at level-60 pay (gold and **Mentor Marks**).
