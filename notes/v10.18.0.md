**Highlights**
- Your realm now feels lived in: the same players on every character, with friends, a player card, and chat where people answer each other by name.
- Bots now think before they act: they use their whole kit, heal smarter and kick red bars in turn, so Normal now hits harder.
- Stacks now hold 20, level-60 rewards fit level 60, and fight logs can go with a bug report.

**The Living Realm**
- Every character on your account now sees the same players. Older saves join the oldest character's realm.
- Added a **Friends** list in **Social**, with who is online and where.
- Added the player card: tap a player in the scene, the guild list, chat or the group pop, or hold a party frame.
- The player card now shows their gear, guild, recent levels and clears, runs with you, ninja rolls, friends and rival.
- Joining someone's group from chat, or buying from them, now makes them a friend.
- Friends no longer quit. They go on a break for a few days, say when they're back, and whisper you on return.
- Players now keep their gear between groups and get new gear when they level.
- Everyone now has their own way of typing, and guildmates, friends and rivals answer each other by name.
- Players now talk about their own week: the level they reached, the dungeon they cleared, the drop they got.
- Trades, craft orders, guild nights and group posts now come from players at the right level for them.
- Newcomers now wait a week before joining a guild, and guilds refill from newcomers who stay.
- Up to 80 players are now level 60, and the levelling zones are as busy as before.

**New Content**
- Added fight logs: **Share fight log** (app) or **Save fight log** (browser) in the Deaths window and **Report a bug**.
- Added **Longnight**'s own music for the festival menu, its towns and its runs.

**Changes**
- Bots now choose from every ability they know; skilled bots choose well more often.
- Healer bots now pick the heal that fits, not just the biggest one.
- One bot in each group now takes the kicks on red bars, so fewer slams on the tank go unanswered.
- At level 60, **Dungeon Roulette**, bounties and a Trial's final boss now pay level-60 gear for your class.
- At level 60, bounties now pay **Mentor Marks** instead of XP.
- Stacks now hold 20 in the bags and the bank, and full bags keep what fits and say what was left behind.
- **Deserter**: 10 → 5 minutes, none once your group has wiped, and it now applies to leaving a battleground early.
- A group-finder pop now expires after 90 seconds, and a dropped queue tells you.
- Closing the app mid-duel now counts as a loss, wager included.
- Moving your device's clock no longer breaks timers, weekly goals, lockouts or festival dailies.

**Balance**
- Increased enemy damage on Normal: dungeons below 60 and raids ×1.15, **The Sunken Archive** and **Temple of Shal'zua** ×1.25.
  *Dev note: smarter bots made groups much safer, so Normal hits harder to stay a real fight. Enemy health is unchanged.*
- **The Tidecrown Citadel**, Hard: the **Coralheart Colossus** calls 1 **Abyssal Spawn** at half health, not 2.
- **Steady Fuse** items: a little less stat budget for the sure critical hit.

**Interface**
- Added a choice for the **Potion** button: long-press it to pick a potion or **Auto**.
- Bounties, Omens, rare hunts, the Roulette, the featured raid and the world boss now say when they next change.
- Request buttons in chat now say why they're greyed out.
- The auction house sell dialog now shows **Usual price**, **Deposit** and **House cut**, and warns that upgrades are lost.
- Enemies now sit right under the party during a pull, and **Ready**, **Leave** and **Go** stay in view.
- **Welcome back** now shows short rows and closes with a tap outside it.
- Skipping a cutscene now takes two taps instead of three.
- Map place names no longer overlap each other or run off the edge.
- Renamed the trophy title to **the Trophy Hunter**, so it no longer shares its name with King Ghostpelt's.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed the scene breaking ("Something went wrong") after a while at a place with three or more gathering nodes.
- Fixed a long time away emptying the realm.
- Fixed leaving a cleared run deleting loot you hadn't rolled on.
- Fixed being in a dungeon run and a battleground at once.
- Fixed a failed cutscene leaving the game paused until a reload.
- Fixed over a hundred smaller problems with the shared realm, chat requests, guilds, battlegrounds and group runs.

**From earlier patches**
- Reduced enemy damage in **The Snuffed Road** (Lanternwake) by about 15%, on Normal and Hard.
