Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- On Android, Realm of Loner moves to a new app. This beta tests the move before everyone gets it.
- Your characters come over with **Cloud save** or a save code. The old app keeps them until you uninstall it.

**Android App**
- The game on Android is now a new app. Updating installs it next to the old one, and both are called **Realm of Loner**.
- The new app's first launch asks **Played before?** and offers your old characters back.
- To move your characters:
  1. In the old app, turn on **Hero → Settings → Cloud save** and let it sync.
  2. Open the new app and tap **Restore from Google Drive**, with the same Google account. Your characters come back.
  3. Once they're there, uninstall the old app. Its **Check for updates** button shows an older version.
- No Google Play services on your phone? Use **Settings → Save → Copy save code** in the old app and **Load save code** in the new one, one character at a time. Mentor Marks, titles, codes and the **Account bank** don't come with it.
- The new app starts with **Beta updates** off. Turn it on again in **Settings** to keep getting test builds.
- The browser version doesn't change.

*Dev note: Android now asks every app to be registered by its developer, and the old one couldn't be. The new app is registered, so installing and updating it gets easier.*

**Known Issues**
- Please tell me in #beta-builds if the move loses anything, or if the new app won't install next to the old one.
