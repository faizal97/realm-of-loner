Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Honor ranks**
- PvP now has a ladder of **8 ranks** for each faction, earned with lifetime Honor from battlegrounds and War Mode. Honor is never lost and never spent, so a rank you reach is yours for good.
- Every rank is a title (Accord / Krugar):
  - Rank 1 at 100 Honor: Recruit / Whelp
  - Rank 2 at 500 Honor: Shieldbearer / Bloodied
  - Rank 3 at 1,500 Honor: Banneret / Raider
  - Rank 4 at 4,000 Honor: Lancer / Tusker
  - Rank 5 at 8,000 Honor: Warden of the Line / Ironhide
  - Rank 6 at 16,000 Honor: Lantern Captain / Warbringer
  - Rank 7 at 32,000 Honor: High Guard / Skullbearer
  - Rank 8 at 64,000 Honor: Lord of the Accord / Hand of the Krugar
- The **PvP** tab shows where you stand, for example "Rank 3 of 8: Banneret. 2,600 of 4,000 Honor to Rank 4: Lancer". Tap it to see the whole ladder.
- **Insignia:** a rank badge sits next to each name on the battleground scoreboard and on party frames, for you and for bots.
- **New looks** at ranks 3, 5 and 8, worn on the back in your faction's colours and collected in the wardrobe for your whole account. For the Accord: Banneret's Tabard, Cloak of the Line and War Banner of the Accord. For the Krugar: Raider's Tabard, Ironhide Cloak and War Banner of the Krugar.
- Ranks give titles, insignia and looks only, never power.
- **Your old Honor titles carry over:** the four you could earn before are now ranks 1 to 4 with their new names, and you keep any you already have.
- Bots have ranks too. About half are unranked, and the higher ranks get rarer the further up you go: a rank-8 bot is a rare sight.
