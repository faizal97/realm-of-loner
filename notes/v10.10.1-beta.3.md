Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Buffs and debuffs**
- Buff icons never shrink into slivers: whatever doesn't fit folds into "+N".
- The full list shows every description in full, with a × at the top to close it.
- Debuffs are boxed in red, in the list and on the frames.

**The scene**
- The scene keeps a strip along its top for the place name, "N online", the battle speed and the menu. No character's name, health bar or sprite is ever drawn into it.
