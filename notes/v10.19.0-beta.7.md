Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- New: **Season's Finest**. Each season names the best piece for every slot of your class and role. Hunt them down, or ask for a hint.
- Group finder players now wear gear that fits their level, so a tank no longer dies to a boss's ordinary hits.
- Dungeons and raids were retuned around them: enemies have more health, fights last longer, and par times moved with them.
- Fixed spent **Mentor Marks** coming back after a relaunch.

**Season's Finest**
- Added **Season's Finest** (Hero → Journey): for each slot of your class and role, a **Best** piece and a **Next best** piece, each Found, Seen or Unknown.
- **Hint** names where an Unknown piece drops; **Reveal** names the item, the boss and the drop chance.
- A piece you find before using Hint or Reveal is **Found blind**. Find every Best piece of a role blind, on any class, to earn that role's title: **the Tideward** (tank), **the Tidemender** (healer) or **the Tidebreaker** (damage).
- **Pathfinder** counts every class and role you've finished blind, across all your characters.
- Every class and role now has a **Best** and a **Next best** piece in every slot, ranged included for hunters and bards.
- Added 52 Season's Finest pieces, stats only: Best pieces drop in the Tidecrown Citadel and in older raids and dungeons, Next best pieces in the older ones.
- Every boss with Season's Finest pieces now rolls for one of them on each kill, on top of its usual loot. Its own loot and drop chances don't change.
- Warrior tanks and damage dealers, and priest healers and damage dealers, now have lists of their own.
- Season's Finest pieces carry a crest wherever you see them.
- Each faction has its own lists, from places your side can enter: the Accord's never name the Temple of Shal'zua, the Krugar's never name the Sunken Archive.
- Only pieces you get during the season can be **Found blind**. Pieces you already had, and pieces from the **Account bank**, count as Found.
- A **Hint** or **Reveal** is on the piece: it ends Found blind for that piece on every role of your class, and the warning names the titles it ends.
- Once a role's title can't be earned on a character, its page says so.
- Reveal shows the drop chance per kill; the group then rolls for the piece.
- Season 1, the **Season of the Tides**, starts on 12 November and runs until the next chapter of the story.
- Upgrade arrows now weigh what warrior and paladin tanks, and paladin and shaman healers, really use.

**Group Finder**
- Group finder players now wear gear in every slot, with stats for their role, and healers and casters (shamans and bards too) have spell power.
- Mage and shaman players now wear **of the Eagle** gear, so they have the health and mana their class stats lack.
- A careless player (one who plays badly) wears whatever dropped, with Common legs, hands and waist, so a bad draw still costs your group something. Raids still take them only in their gear.
- Raids now take players in Rare gear on Normal and Epic gear on Hard.
- *Dev note: a level-60 tank from the group finder had about 2,100 health, against a boss whose ordinary hits land for 1,100–1,800. Now it has about 3,800.*

**Dungeons and Raids**
- Increased enemy health in every dungeon and raid: dungeons below 60 about ×1.7, level-60 dungeons about ×1.9, raids ×1.5–2. Damage is unchanged.
- Increased par times to match the longer runs, so the speed bonus is as reachable as before.
- Trials keep their numbers.

**Items**
- Upgrading an item with **Mentor Marks** now raises its damage and armour toward the best raid piece of its kind, never past it.
- **Hard** raid drops now go 2 upgrade steps past that, so they are always stronger than the Normal drop.
- Items you already upgraded are rebuilt once at the power you paid for, including items in the account bank.

- Reduced **Opening Cut**'s extra damage at level 60: 124 → 100.
- Increased **Glass Heart**'s damage bonus: 2.5% → 2.7%.
- Reduced **Tithe of Battle**'s mana: 0.3% → 0.15% per tick, and its items keep a little less of their stats (cost 40% → 45%). Longer boss fights made it far too strong for a warlock.
- **Stubborn Blood** and **Steady Fuse** items keep a little less of their stats (cost 56% → 60%).
- *Dev note: these effects were checked again against the new, longer fights.*
