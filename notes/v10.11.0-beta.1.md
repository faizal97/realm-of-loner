Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Android: smoother, we hope**
- The app uses a smoother way of drawing the game. If it feels worse for you, you can switch back to the old mode: tap the version on the title screen eight times quickly. The change applies the next time you open the app.
- **Frame-rate counter:** tap the version on the title screen five times quickly to show or hide a small counter in the corner, with frames per second and the slowest frame. One tap still opens What's new.
- **Please tell us** whether the game feels smoother or worse on your phone, in #beta-builds or with Report a bug. The numbers from the counter help a lot.

**Bags**
- The Bags tab warns you before your bags are full: an amber number when 3 or fewer slots are free, and a red "Full" at 0. The Bags header shows the free slots too, and so does Bags in the run menu.

**Fixes**
- War Mode alerts in chat name the Accord and the Krugar.
- The Daily Roulette resets at your own midnight, as its message says. If you already did one today, that one still counts until it resets, and from your next run it follows your own days.
- Two pop-up messages at once no longer cover each other: they stack, the newest below, at most three at a time, and the same message isn't shown twice.
