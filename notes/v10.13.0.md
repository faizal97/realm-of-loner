**Highlights**
- New festival: **Lanternwake**, starting **24 October** (not yet): every year until 7 November, with its own 5-player run, **The Snuffed Road**.
- A class update for every class: **Warriors** and **Priests** can queue as Damage, **Paladins** get a taunt, and many class bugs are fixed.
- New **Database** website with an official guide for every class, opened from **Settings → Guides**.

**Lanternwake (starts 24 October)**
- Lanternwake is in the game now but opens on **24 October** and runs to 7 November. Until then you can't earn candles or enter The Snuffed Road.
- Added **Lanternwake**, Caldreth's autumn festival. No power, only looks and fun.
- Added **Wake Candles**, from kills, quests, dungeon bosses and **Light the Way** (up to 160 a day).
- Added **Wakekeeper Ondine** to every town with a profession trainer.
- Added the **Lanternwake Robe**, **Trousers** and **Wakelight Lantern** (looks only), **Flicker**, a companion, and the title **the Lantern-Bearer**.
- Added **The Snuffed Road** (5 players, levels 10–60, fought at your level), with **Hard** after one clear.
- Added the **Wickless Steed**: the **Wickless Knight** can drop it each day; the 10th daily kill always does.
- Added a festival waltz for **The Snuffed Road** and the title screen while Lanternwake is on.
- Added **Hero → Journey → Events** and the event lantern, today's festival progress on every screen.

**All Classes**
- Casters behind a tank now step in for close-range spells like **Frost Nova** and **Magma Totem**, then step back.
- Slows now work at a quarter of their strength on bosses.
- Shields now stack: a second shield no longer replaces the first.
- Fixed shields taking their mana outside a fight and doing nothing.
- Fixed a talent reaction lighting abilities you haven't learned.
- Fixed several tooltips that didn't match what the ability does.

**Druid**
- **Barkskin** and **Frenzied Regeneration** now work in **Bear Form**, and **Bash** and **Feral Charge** now show on its bar.
- **Innervate** now keeps your mana refilling while you cast.
- Bear tanks in your group now use **Swipe**, **Bash**, **Feral Charge**, **Barkskin** and **Frenzied Regeneration**.
- Fixed **Thorns** sometimes not striking back, or striking back under the wrong name.

**Hunter**
- Only one of **Spirit of the Hawk** and **Spirit of the Monkey** can be on at a time.

**Mage**
- **Arcane Blast**: each cast in the last 6 sec makes the next cost 50% more mana, up to 3 times.
- *Dev note: Arcane Blast did more than twice Fireball's damage for less mana. Weave the two now.*
- **Ice Block** now stops you casting, moving and attacking; use it again to break out early.
- Fixed **Arcane Missiles** not using **Arcane Surge**, and its missiles not triggering reactions.

**Paladin**
- Added **Hand of Challenge** (level 12): a taunt with a 10 sec cooldown.
- **Holy Shock** now heals your heal target when you are the healer, and hurts your enemy otherwise.
- **Improved Oaths** (was Improved Oath of Righteousness) and **Benediction** now cover **Oath of Command** too.
- Fixed **Holy Shield** absorbs not counting toward threat.

**Priest**
- **Priests** can now queue as Damage in the group finder.
- Casting a heal now ends **Shadowform**.
- **Flash Heal**: healing at level 60 about 405 → 555.
- **Vampiric Embrace**: cost at level 60 80 → 316 mana.
- Fixed **Improved Word of Fortitude** not raising **Prayer of Fortitude**.

**Rogue**
- **Backstab**: cost 60 → 50 energy, bonus damage at level 60 105 → 150.
- **Rupture** at 5 combo points: damage at level 60 about 525 → 255.
- *Dev note: Rupture now grows with each combo point, as its tooltip always said, so its base came down to match.*
- Fixed **Rupture** dealing full damage at any number of combo points.

**Shaman**
- **Chain Heal** now jumps to the 2 most hurt allies within 12 m, for half and a quarter.
- **Chain Heal**: cost at level 60 445 → 534 mana.
- Fixed a weapon imbue not adding to your weapon shot.
- Fixed **Lightning Shield** sometimes not striking back, or striking back under the wrong name.

**Warlock**
- Only one of **Demon Skin** and **Demon Armor** can be on at a time.

**Warrior**
- **Warriors** can now queue as Damage in the group finder.
- Fixed **Cleave** never lighting **Battle Fury**.
- Fixed **Retaliation** sometimes not striking back, or striking back under the wrong name.

**World**
- A creature that joins your fight from nearby now deals 75% damage below level 20.
- Chance that a nearby creature joins your fight from level 20: 14% → 12%.
- *Dev note: solo Mages and Priests were dying too often when a second creature joined. The creature you pulled is unchanged.*

**Interface**
- Added **Settings → Guides**: your class guide and the Database, opened in your browser.
- Added class crests to character creation, character select and the hero sheet.
- Character creation now shows every role a class can take.
- Added a small **i** to rows with more detail: tap the row for a one-line explanation.
- Help text on the Quest Log, group finder, War Mode, Honor, Trophies, Professions, cooking, bounties and Settings is now shorter.
