Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Everyone on the realm now talks in their own way, and people who know each other answer each other in chat.
- Players now talk about their own week: the level they just reached, the dungeon they cleared, the drop they got.
- Trades, craft orders and guild nights now come from players at the right level for them.

**Changes**
- Each player now has their own way of typing: words they start or end with, and their own shorthand.
- Guildmates, friends and rivals now answer each other in **Guild**, **General** and **LFG**, by name. Now and then the first one answers back.
- Players now mention what they did in the last few days: a level reached, a dungeon cleared, an item they got, a guild they joined.
- **Guild** and party chat now have many more lines, so you hear the same one far less often.
- Selling, swapping, buying materials, craft orders and gear questions now only come from players at that item's level.
- A guild night is now announced by someone at the dungeon's level.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed a guildmate announcing a level they hadn't reached yet.
