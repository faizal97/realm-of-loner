Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Bots now think before they act: they use their whole kit, pick the best ability for the moment, and kick red bars in turn.
- Level-60 rewards fit level 60: Roulette, bounties and a Trial's final boss pay level-60 gear.
- Fight logs: save or share the fight you lost and send it with a bug report.

**New Content**
- Added fight logs: **Share fight log** (app) or **Save fight log** (browser) in the Deaths window and in **Report a bug**.
- Added **Longnight**'s own music for the festival menu, its towns and its runs.

**Changes**
- Bots now choose from every ability they know, scored for the moment; skilled bots choose well more often.
- Healer bots now look ahead and pick the heal that fits, not just the biggest one.
- One bot in each group now takes the kicks on red bars, so fewer slams on the tank go unanswered.
- The Bard healer bot now sings **Hearthsong** and **Anthem of Stone** only when the group is safe and its mana is high.
- At level 60, the **Dungeon Roulette** bonus is now a level-60 blue for your class.
- At level 60, bounties pay **Mentor Marks** instead of XP, and the weekly bonus is a level-60 blue for your class.
- A Trial's final boss now drops its blue and better items at level 60.
- **Deserter**: 10 → 5 minutes, and none once your group has wiped.

**Balance**
- Increased enemy damage on Normal: dungeons below 60 and raids ×1.15, **The Sunken Archive** and **Temple of Shal'zua** ×1.25.
  *Dev note: smarter bots made groups much safer, so Normal hits harder to stay a real fight. Enemy health is unchanged.*
- Raid **Hard** stays just above the new Normal.
- **The Tidecrown Citadel**, Hard: the **Coralheart Colossus** calls 1 **Abyssal Spawn** at half health, not 2.
- **Steady Fuse** items: a little less stat budget for the sure critical hit.
  *Dev note: thinking bots get more from a sure crit, so it costs a bit more of the item's stats.*

**Interface**
- Added a choice for the **Potion** button: long-press it to pick a potion or **Auto** (back to Auto when it runs out).
- Bounties, Omens, rare hunts, the Roulette, the featured raid and the world boss now say when they next change.
- **Omens** now say they change on the 1st, 8th, 15th and 22nd, not "every week".

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed the scene breaking ("Something went wrong") after a while at a place with three or more gathering nodes.
- Fixed bot chat getting the time of day or the server's mood wrong, remembering you from nowhere, and repeating itself.
