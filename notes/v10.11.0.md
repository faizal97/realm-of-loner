Better gear while you level, a game that tells you why a tap did nothing, nothing lost to full bags, a death recap, and talents every class can finish. This update also brings everything since v10.10.4.

**Gear before level 60**
- A blue is now clearly better than a green of its level (about 20% more), and generated greens and blues roll their stats a little higher or lower (up to 10% either way), so a good find and a poor one both exist. Gear stats no longer dip at level 60.
- Dungeon blues are never weaker than a random blue of their level.
- Quest rewards for gear offer **two versions** with different stat mixes in the same armour type. Tap **Choose** on the one you want (it then reads "Chosen ✓"). Weapons can be any type your class uses, bows included.
- From level 30, a dungeon's last boss has a 2% chance and a rare creature 5% of dropping a level-appropriate epic that fits your class. Expect one to three in a whole levelling journey. Level-60 gear is unchanged.
- Armour names now say what they are made of: Stitched Leather (was Handstitched), Silk (was Soft) and Linked (was Rusted). Items you already own keep their names.

**Talents at level 60**
- Rogue, Warrior and Mage earned more talent points than their trees could hold: 7, 3 and 3 points could never be spent. Each now has new talents, so every class can spend all 51.
- Rogue: Quick Finish (Assassination), Precise Strikes (Combat) and Shadowed Step (Subtlety). Warrior: Heavy Swings (Arms). Mage: Biting Cold (Frost).
- Your saved talents stay as they are, and the points you couldn't spend before are free to use.
- The effects Stubborn Blood and Steady Fuse were retuned alongside the new talents, so their items give up a little more or less of their stats.

**PvP and wipes**
- Every PvP fight (duels, the Bloodsand Brawl, battlegrounds and War Mode ambushes) now runs at normal speed, whatever your battle speed is set to, and the speed chip is hidden in all of them. Your setting comes back in the next fight against monsters. The first PvP fight of a session tells you.
- After a wipe in a dungeon or raid, and after you die in the open world, a recap lists who died and what killed them (the last hit and its size). Under each name: how much damage they took in the last 5 seconds, how long before the death their last heal landed, and the healer's state at that moment (for example out of mana or dead). It only states facts, so you can see what happened.
- Tap **Deaths** between pulls to open it again. If you died yourself, it opens straight away.

**A tap that can't act now says why**
- A greyed button gives its reason on the button itself, in a dashed outline so it reads as "not now" rather than broken: "Queued", "In queue for <activity>", "Bags full", "Need 2g 40s" (the exact shortfall), "Learn Riding first", "You're home".
- The action bar dims while you travel or are dead, and a tap says why ("You're travelling." / "You're dead. You revive in 60 sec."). Cooldowns keep ticking on it.
- Tapping a gathering node in a fight says "Finish the fight first." At a vendor, items the vendor won't buy dim, and a tap says "The vendor won't buy <item>."
- "Not ready" and "Busy" are gone. You now see "Still casting <spell>" or "<spell> isn't ready (12s)".
- A short buff used out of a fight (Bloodlust, Evasion, Rallying Cry and similar) is refused with "Use it in a fight." It no longer spends mana or rage and starts the cooldown for nothing.

**You see what really happened**
- **Selling:** a "Last sale" line in the vendor's header shows what you sold and for how much, and your money flashes gold. Green items or better, and anything marked as an upgrade, ask first ("Sell <item>?" with **Keep** and **Sell**). Greys and whites still sell in one tap.
- **Create all** that stops because your bags filled says so: "Bags full. Made 3 of 10." A refused cook no longer shows "Perfect!" or "Burnt" on top of the refusal.
- **Loot rolls:** the timer bar goes grey and says "Paused while you fight" or "Paused while you look" while it waits. If it runs out, the Loot log says "Time ran out: you passed on <item>."
- **Queue:** "Queued 3m" shows in your frame during a fight too, and tapping it opens your queue.
- **Quest items you pick up:** the quest objective says where, for example "Collect them here" or "Collect them in Halden Vineyards" (or "Dropped by <creature>" for a drop).
- A bounty you've taken opens its details (where to hunt, progress).

**Nothing is lost to full bags**
- A loot roll you win with full bags goes to your bank, with a message naming the item.
- Taking a craft order with full bags waits: you're told to make room for the materials first and the order stays open. Bots no longer say the materials are in a mailbox.
- Taking a stack out of the bank with full bags and a full stack no longer deletes it; it stays in the bank.
- World loot with full bags: green items and better go to your bank with a message; grey and white items that don't fit are named in one line per kill instead of vanishing.

**Healing and groups**
- The Party header reads **"Healing: you"** or **"Healing: <name>"** (in their class colour), with "tap a member to heal them" when nobody is picked. It shows for any class with a heal aimed at an ally. Your pick stays between pulls, and tapping it again sends heals back to you.
- Tap a member's row and it highlights straight away, in the world and in dungeons. A Shaman's top frame switches to the member you picked, like a Priest's. If that member dies or leaves, you get one yellow notice instead of a silent switch.
- Between pulls in a dungeon or raid, each row shows a green **✓** once that member is ready and a thin mana bar. The status line above **Ready** says who the group is waiting for ("Waiting for <name>'s mana (32% of 45%)") and turns green when everyone is ready. **Ready** reads "Ready ✓" once you tap it.
- Tanks no longer say "ready?" in party chat before a pull.
- Chat requests show the time they have left at the end of the line ("4m", then "<1m"). When one runs out it stays in chat, greyed, and reads "expired". Help and guild applications close only if they worked, and a refusal says why. A group that already found someone shows "Someone else took this spot".

**Quests and levelling**
- Quest XP now falls once you are well past a quest: full XP up to 2 levels over it, then 20% less for each level beyond that, down to 20%. Kills already worked this way. The quest log shows it before you take the quest: "(reduced: you're 4 levels over)".
- A few quests that sat in the wrong zone, 10 levels or more above it, were moved to where they belong.
- Creatures below level 20 bring a friend into a fight half as often (7% of pulls, was 14%), and the friend arrives after 4–7 seconds instead of 2–5. A creature that is going to join shows in the fight list straight away, marked "joins in 4 s". Solo Mages were dying in these fights around level 15 and now die much less. Some fights at levels 15 to 25 are still too hard for Mages and Priests, and that continues next.
- The bounty board no longer counts lapsed bounties toward the limit of 6. Old bounties leave your character when you load it or take a new one.

**Fixes**
- Bear Form no longer drops back to your normal form a few seconds into a fight, and your rage no longer resets to 0 with it, so you can build enough for Maul and the other bear attacks. The cause was the automatic save, which shifted you out of your form during fights.
