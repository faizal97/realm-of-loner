Test build, browser only: play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- New when questing: **Pull the group**, for creatures standing together as a pack.
- The group finder now shows who you're about to play with.
- Bard healers in your groups keep their mana for healing.

**New Content**
- Added **Pull the group**: a pack of 2 (3 from creature level 20) comes at once, each with half the health, damage and XP.
- *Dev note: a pack is faster with area attacks and riskier without them. Tapping the creature still pulls one.*

**Changes**
- A healer **Bard** in your group now starts **Song of Rest** when 3 allies are hurt, not 1, and plays **Marching Song** only with mana to spare.

**Interface**
- **Your group is ready** now lists the other players: name, level, class, role, and **Friend** or **Guild** when you know them.
- In a raid it shows how many of each role, with a row for anyone you know.
- **Enter** is now refused mid-fight: "Finish the fight first."

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed **The Snuffed Road**'s first pull reading "Lantern-wisps".

**Known Issues**
- Pack pulls are new: numbers may change before the release.
