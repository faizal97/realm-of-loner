Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Added a **Friends** list in **Social**, with who is online and where.
- Friends on a break now say when they're back, and whisper you when they return.
- Many fixes to the shared realm, the player card, chat requests and group runs.

**Changes**
- Joining someone's group from chat, or buying from them, now makes them a friend. Trials and battlegrounds too.
- Newcomers now wait a week before joining a guild, so guilds no longer fill with people who quit at once.
- Guilds now refill from newcomers who stay.
- General and guild chat now keep a steady pace all hour.
- Players who post a group are now about the dungeon's level, and the one who posts a **Help Wanted** is in the group.
- The day's **Dungeon Roulette** dungeon now stays the same if you leave and start again.
- The weekly guild goal now pays once a week, even if you change guild.
- Reloading in the middle of a pull now counts as a wipe.
- Leaving a group with loot still out now rolls it at once. Your open rolls count as a pass.
- A realm rebuilt on a new device now comes from your most recent character.
- Save codes are now shorter: they leave out the realm's history, which your cloud save still keeps.

**Interface**
- Added **Remove friend** warnings: a friend no other character has may quit the realm.
- The player card now opens from the group pop, and the run waits while it's open.
- **Invite** on the player card now says why when it can't be used, and shows the answer on screen.
- **Group ready** now shows in Social and on the main screen if you close the pop.
- Tapping a party frame now opens the player card when you have no heals to aim.
- **Welcome back** now shows short rows: Away, Rested, Players levelled, New players, Online now.
- **Haggle** now works once per offer, then shows the answer.
- **Apply** on a guild's recruit post now shows the guild's level when you're too low.
- Skipping a cutscene now takes two taps instead of three.
- The festival lantern no longer shows during runs and battlegrounds, or over menus and dialogs.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed deleting a character sometimes giving two players the same identity.
- Fixed a long time away emptying the realm.
- Fixed your other characters' guilds emptying without a goodbye.
- Fixed a deleted character's friends never quitting.
- Fixed switching characters closing every open chat request.
- Fixed a save code or cloud pull showing the wrong person's runs on the player card.
- Fixed a new character named like a friend splitting that friend into two people.
- Fixed newcomers taking one of your characters' names.
- Fixed a new character losing its welcome chat on its first reload.
- Fixed the character list and the save disagreeing when the device's storage is full. You now get a message.
- Fixed moving the device clock back making the realm live the same days twice.
- Fixed players' friends and rivals coming from the enemy faction.
- Fixed the player card naming content above your level.
- Fixed **Invite** reaching players in other zones.
- Fixed losing the group pop after opening a player card from it.
- Fixed **Remove friend** letting a run pull while it was open.
- Fixed joining a run from chat leaving **Social** over the fight.
- Fixed a story cutscene playing over a dialog you had just opened.
- Fixed the loot roll timer running during a story cutscene.
- Fixed leaving a cleared run deleting loot you hadn't rolled on.
- Fixed one unanswered party invite blocking every later invite.
- Fixed being in a dungeon run and a battleground at once.
- Fixed a failed cutscene leaving the game paused until a reload.
- Fixed a help or team-up task still paying after the helper left your party.
- Fixed a team-up for a quest you'd already finished paying at once.
- Fixed leaving your guild during a guild task leaving that guildmate in your party.
- Fixed **No thanks** to a whispered guild invite not stopping that guild asking again.
- Fixed a thank-you gift getting a "hello?" nudge and a goodbye.
- Fixed taken tasks, signed-up guild events and taken craft orders counting as open requests.
- Fixed a party member tapping your mob.
- Fixed the second mob on a party member vanishing when your target died.
- Fixed **Word of Fortitude** cast between pulls buffing only you.
- Fixed players thanking you for a buff you never cast.
- Fixed "Dungeons are open" showing at level 8 with no dungeon to join.
- Fixed the player card's Where: your town during a run, Offline in the group pop.
- Fixed runs with players from other realms not counting as runs together.
- Fixed an empty box on some friends' cards.
- Fixed "saw u fighting" whispers before you'd fought anything.
- Fixed goodbye lines saying "for now" from players who never come back.
