A layout patch: more room on a phone screen, fights that stay still, an easier way around during dungeons and battlegrounds, and bots that keep story secrets.

**A roomier main screen**
- The header, the scene and the chat strip are slimmer, so the panel below gets about twice the room on a phone.
- The header gives you and your target one line each, with buffs in the name row. With no target, "Rested" and "In queue" show as small chips.
- The chat strip shows the newest line; tap it for the full chat.
- In a dungeon fight your party's frames sit at the top of the panel, so a healer sees all 5 at once. Between pulls, the frames, Pull/Leave and the pace choice are all on screen.

**Fights stay still**
- The action bar never changes size when a fight starts or ends, so every button stays where your thumb expects it. Out of a fight, Eat and Drink take the first fight-only slots.
- Every panel puts what you decide first: in a run, the party, Pull/Leave and the pace, then the clock and progress. In a battleground, the score, the three banners and your picks, then your team.
- Loot rolls sit in a one-line card above the action bar (item, its effect, time left, Need/Greed/Pass) instead of covering the panel.

**Easier to get around**
- Menus stop above the bottom tabs, so you can go from Bags to Hero in one tap.
- The middle tab for quest givers is now called **Here**.
- The action bar's handle is bigger to touch, and **Arrange** is right on the bar.
- In a dungeon, a raid, a Trial, a battleground or the Bloodsand Brawl, the bottom tabs hide for the whole run to give the fight more room. A menu button at the scene's top-left, under the place name, opens Bags, Hero, Quests and Social, with a dot when something needs you. It and the battle speed never cover an enemy's name. A tip says where they went the first time.

**Clearer**
- Tap an Honor rank badge to see the rank, its name, whose it is and the Honor it takes, with a button to see the whole ladder.
- Long item names in the Hero equipment grid take two lines instead of being cut off, and a tap on your name in the header shows it in full with your title.
- Bots in chat talk about places and bosses at their own level, and never give away a story secret you haven't reached.

**Fixes**
- "1 new adventurer started out" (was "adventurers").
- War Mode's Honor reads with a comma (16,500), like the rank line.
