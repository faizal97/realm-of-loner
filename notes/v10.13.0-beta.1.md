Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Lanternwake, Caldreth's autumn festival**
On Lanternwake night Caldreth lights candles for its dead, so they can find the road home. In the live game it runs every year from **24 October to 7 November**; in this beta it is on now so you can try it.
- **Wake Candles** come from kills that still teach you something (not grey), quests, dungeon bosses, **Light the Way** (light a lantern at 5 places out in the world, from the Fight tab) and the Wickless Knight. Up to **160 a day**, about 40 minutes of play. They vanish when the festival ends.
- **Wakekeeper Ondine** stands in every town with a profession trainer. She sells the **Lanternwake Robe, Trousers and Wakelight Lantern** (looks only, no stats), **Flicker**, a little wisp that follows you, and the title **the Lantern-Bearer**. She also sells things to spend candles on afterwards: costumes for an hour, treats to toss to people in town, and lanterns to hang in a town.
- **The Snuffed Road**: a 5-player run from the group finder, fought at your own level from 10 to 60. A pack of wisps, then the **Wickless Knight**: heavy blows on his target, a gallop that hits the whole group, wisps that join at low health, and at Midnight he hits much harder. His loot drops once a day. Every daily kill can drop the **Wickless Steed**, and the 10th always does. After one clear, **Hard** drops a darker version of a set piece once a day.
- **Everything in one place**: Hero → Journey → Events → Lanternwake shows the story, today's candles, the run, every reward and its price.
- **The event lantern** floats on every screen while the festival is on. Its ring fills with today's candles and a dot shows while something daily is still open. Tap it for today's progress, drag it to either edge, or hide it in Settings.
- The title screen wears the festival: a night sky with lanterns drifting up.

**About beta rewards**
The beta shares your characters with the live game, so what you earn in this test festival stays in beta builds. Open the same character in the live game, or wait for the real festival to start on 24 October, and the test candles, looks, Flicker, the title and the Steed go away. Everyone starts the real Lanternwake the same.
