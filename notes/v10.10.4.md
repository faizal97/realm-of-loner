Small fixes and volume sliders.

This update is out now in the browser, on itch.io and the game's own page. The Android app gets it with the next feature update.

**Sound**
- Hero → Settings → Sound has volume sliders for **Music** and **Effects**, from off to 100%. 100% sounds as before.

**Fixes**
- If you die at the same moment as your killing blow, you no longer get stuck: the kill counts, and you live on at 1 health.
- Back on a quest or bounty page takes you back to the Quest Log once, instead of stacking another Quest Log on top.
- Handing in a bounty says "Bounty complete" with what it paid (XP, money, Mentor Marks), instead of "Quest complete".
- A weekly bounty that gives bonus gear waits until you have a free bag slot, so the gear is never lost. With full bags it asks you to make room first and pays nothing until you do.
- The cast bar fills smoothly instead of in small jumps. Cast times are unchanged.
- In the Rimebone Den, the icicles hang from the cave mouth where they belong.
- The Gates of Gearhollow, the Thorn Warrens and the Magma Throne now show on their zone maps.
