Fewer things lost, fewer things hidden: quest items are safe from full bags, every quest shows where to hand it in, Rumhook Bay and Saltmarsh get their own maps, and you can set the music and sound volume.

**Quests**
- Items a quest asks you to collect are kept with the quest, not in your bags, so you can pick them up with full bags and never lose them. They go when you turn the quest in, or if you abandon it.
- Every quest page says where to turn it in from the start (who and where), with **Show the way**.
- A quest or weekly bounty whose reward is an item waits until you have a free bag slot: with full bags it asks you to make room first and pays nothing until you do, so the item is never lost.
- Handing in a bounty says "Bounty complete" with what it paid (XP, money, Mentor Marks).

**Maps**
- Rumhook Bay and Saltmarsh have their own maps, with every place on them. The Vinewild's map has a **Rumhook Bay** marker at its south edge that opens it.
- The Gates of Gearhollow, the Thorn Warrens and the Magma Throne now show on their zone maps.

**Sound**
- Hero → Settings → Sound has volume sliders for **Music** and **Effects**, from off to 100%. 100% sounds as before.

**Professions**
- You can unlearn a profession from the Professions screen, for free, anywhere. The confirmation says what you lose (your skill and the recipes you know) and what you keep (keepsakes, titles and the items you made or gathered). You can learn it again from a trainer, from skill 1.
- Cooking and Fishing can't be unlearned, since they never take a profession slot.

**Balance**
- Early fights at levels 4 to 7 last a little longer, so you get to use more of your abilities. Levels 1 to 3 are unchanged.

**Fixes**
- If you die at the same moment as your killing blow, you no longer get stuck: the kill counts, and you live on at 1 health.
- Back on a quest or bounty page takes you back to the Quest Log once, instead of stacking another Quest Log on top.
- The cast bar fills smoothly instead of in small jumps. Cast times are unchanged.
- In the Rimebone Den, the icicles hang from the cave mouth where they belong.
