Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Healing a party member is clear now**
- The Party header says where your heals go: **"Heals go to: you"** or **"Heals go to: <name>"**. With nobody picked it reads "Heals go to: you · tap a member to heal them". It shows for any class with a heal aimed at an ally (Priest, Paladin, Shaman, Druid); everyone else still sees "XP is shared".
- Tap a member's row and it gets its green border straight away, in the world and in dungeons. A Shaman's top frame now switches to the member you picked, like a Priest's.
- Your pick **stays between pulls** while that member is still in the party and alive. Tap the picked row again to send heals back to you.
- If the member you picked dies or leaves, you get one message ("<name> died. Heals go to you.") instead of a silent switch.

**The rest between pulls shows what the group is waiting for**
- In a dungeon or raid, each member's row shows a **✓** once their health and mana are high enough for the pace, and a thin **mana bar** under their health. Both only show between pulls.
- The status line now sits right above the Ready button and names the hold-up: "Waiting for <name>'s mana (32% of 45%)", your own minimum rest, or "Everyone ready". If the wait runs out it says "Pulling anyway."
- After you tap **Ready** the button reads "Ready ✓", so you can tell it counted.
- Tanks no longer say "ready?" in party chat before a pull.

**Nothing is lost to full bags**
- A loot roll you win with full bags goes to your bank, with a message naming the item.
- Taking a craft order with full bags now waits: you're told to make room for the materials first and the order stays open. Bots no longer say the materials are in a mailbox.
- Taking a stack out of the bank with full bags and a full stack no longer deletes it; it stays in the bank.
- World loot with full bags: green items and better go to your bank with a message; grey and white items that don't fit are named in one line per kill instead of vanishing.

**Fixes**
- The bounty board no longer counts lapsed bounties toward the limit of 6. Old bounties leave your character when you load it or take a new one, and the count on the board matches the Quest Log.
- A short buff used out of a fight (Bloodlust, Evasion, Rallying Cry and similar) is now refused with "Use it in a fight." It no longer spends mana or rage and starts the cooldown for nothing.
