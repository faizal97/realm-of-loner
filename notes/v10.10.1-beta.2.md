Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Clearer**
- Buffs and debuffs on your frame, the target frame and party frames show debuffs first (soonest to run out first), then short or fight-related buffs. Only long buffs, like food or a flask, fold into "+N". It turns red if a debuff is hidden in it, and a tap shows the full list.

**Fixes**
- The menu button in dungeons, raids, Trials, battlegrounds and the Bloodsand Brawl now really opens Bags, Hero, Quests and Social. In beta.1 it only showed a note.
- That menu button and the battle speed moved to the scene's top-left, under the place name, so they never cover a boss's name.
