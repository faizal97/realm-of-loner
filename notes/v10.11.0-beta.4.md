Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Maps**
- Rumhook Bay and Saltmarsh have their own maps, with every place on them.
- The Vinewild's map has a **Rumhook Bay** marker at its south edge that opens Rumhook Bay's map.

**Quests**
- Items a quest asks you to collect are kept with the quest, not in your bags, so you can pick them up with full bags and never lose them. They go when you turn the quest in, or if you abandon it.
- Every quest page says where to turn it in from the start (who and where), with **Show the way**.
- A quest whose reward is an item waits until you have a free bag slot: with full bags it asks you to make room first and pays nothing until you do.

**Professions**
- You can unlearn a profession from the Professions screen, for free, anywhere. The confirmation says what you lose (your skill and the recipes you know) and what you keep (keepsakes, titles and the items you made or gathered). You can learn it again from a trainer, from skill 1.
- Cooking and Fishing can't be unlearned, since they never take a profession slot.

**Balance**
- Early fights at levels 4 to 7 last a little longer, so you get to use more of your abilities. Levels 1 to 3 are unchanged.
