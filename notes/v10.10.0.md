The level-60 update: item effects arrive on drops, level-60 rares become hunts with trophies to collect, PvP gets a ladder of Honor ranks, and Priests and Druids level faster.

**Item effects**
- Some drops now carry an **effect**: a twist that reacts to what you do in a fight, paid for with part of the item's stats (an effect item always keeps at least 40%). It's a choice, not a straight upgrade, so bags mark it ◆ instead of ▲.
- 12 effects. Damage: Opening Cut, Kindled Edge, Chase the Next, Steady Fuse, Glass Heart. Healing: Echoing Mend, Lavish Mend, Tethered Mend. Tanking: Turning Guard, Spiteful Hide. Also Tithe of Battle (resources) and Stubborn Blood (survival). Each one is strong in some fights and weak in others.
- Every dungeon's final boss drops one, and so does every raid boss (Hard drops it upgrade steps higher) and every world boss.
- **Trial finds:** a Trial beaten in time has a 1 in 5 chance to drop an effect item as a level-60 blue. It's the dungeon's own find, or another one from this month's Trials that suits your role if you can't use that one or already have it. The briefing shows the chance and the item.
- The item card shows the Effect line with real numbers and its **Effect strength** (100% as dropped). After a run it says what the effect did ("Last run: 1,240 damage, 6% of yours").
- The loot roll, dungeon briefings and boss cards show Effect lines too, and a new effect item you can use lights the Bags dot.
- Mentor Mark upgrades make an effect a little stronger, and the upgrade dialog shows its numbers and strength before and after.
- In a fight, effects with a long cooldown (Stubborn Blood, Steady Fuse) show a callout over your character when they fire, and every effect's damage and healing show in the effect colour.
- The Hero sheet lists the Effects you wear, and **Hero → Journey → Effects** is the codex: all 12, which your account owns, and every item that carries each one, with where it comes from.

**Rare hunts and Trophies (level 60)**
- Each level-60 rare appears once in every 6-hour window and stays up for 30 minutes. When one shows up, someone posts a **Rare sighting** in General (tap it to go there), and the map marks the place with an orange ◆.
- They're a real fight: the non-elite rares hit hard, and the elite ones are made for a full world party of three.
- The first time any of your characters kills a level-60 rare or a world boss, your account takes its **trophy**. There are 11, shown in **Hero → Journey → Trophies**. Take 10 for the title **"the Big-Game Hunter"**.

**Honor ranks**
- PvP has a ladder of **8 ranks** for each faction, earned with lifetime Honor from battlegrounds and War Mode. Honor is never lost and never spent. Every rank is a title (Accord / Krugar):
  - Rank 1 at 100 Honor: Recruit / Whelp
  - Rank 2 at 500 Honor: Shieldbearer / Bloodied
  - Rank 3 at 1,500 Honor: Banneret / Raider
  - Rank 4 at 4,000 Honor: Lancer / Tusker
  - Rank 5 at 8,000 Honor: Warden of the Line / Ironhide
  - Rank 6 at 16,000 Honor: Lantern Captain / Warbringer
  - Rank 7 at 32,000 Honor: High Guard / Skullbearer
  - Rank 8 at 64,000 Honor: Lord of the Accord / Hand of the Krugar
- The **PvP** tab shows where you stand and what's next. A rank badge sits next to names on party frames and your team's line in the battleground, for you and for bots.
- New looks at ranks 3, 5 and 8: a tabard, a cloak and a war banner, worn on the back in your faction's colours. Ranks give titles, insignia and looks only, never power.
- **Your old Honor titles carry over:** they are now ranks 1 to 4 with their new names, and you keep any you already have.

**Priest and Druid level faster**
- **Word of Pain** (Priest) deals about 60% more damage, and **Moonbeam** (Druid) burns for 12 sec instead of 9, about twice as much damage over time. Levelling solo as either is now close to the other classes.

**Bots**
- Bots in groups and world parties carry weapons and capes made for their own level (some carried a level-20 weapon long after level 20).
- From level 50, bots sometimes wear an effect item, about 1 in 4 skilled bots at level 60. It fires just like yours, and you can see it by tapping their frame. Bot groups are a little stronger for both.
- Bots have Honor ranks too, and a rank-8 bot is a rare sight. Bots soloing monsters heal themselves when hurt.

**Friends and cloud save**
- When you turn on Friends with cloud save off, the game asks **"Also back up your characters?"** **Turn on both** does both in one Google window; **Friends only** won't ask again on this device. (Not on itch.io, where cloud save isn't available; save codes move your characters.)

**Balance**
- Every dungeon and raid has a new par time: at Normal pace a group beats it (and wins the speed chest) about 1 run in 4. Trials keep their own par.
- **Fast pace** chains the next pull less often and goes into a boss a little lower on health: faster, and riskier where the fight can punish you.
- Drinks restore their mana in 12 seconds instead of 18.

**Clearer screens**
- Tips about a fight or a run show during it, as a banner your taps pass through.
- The pace choice shows Careful, Normal and Fast side by side: how long each rests, the health and mana it pulls at, and whether it pulls the next group.
- Under the tame buttons, a line says that taming another beast replaces your pet.
- Long messages wrap inside a phone screen, and long names in lists wrap to a second line instead of being cut off.

**Fixes**
- New Hunters no longer start with a Warlock's imp; you tame your first beast at level 10. A Hunter who already has the imp keeps it until then.
- Titles, the wardrobe, Trophies and the group finder no longer name places or people your character hasn't reached in the story. They say "beyond your level" instead.
- The Battle for Highmoor has its own picture in the group finder instead of a dungeon boss's face.
- Section headings no longer run into a long subtitle (the Recipes list in Professions).
- Tarro of Ossa's title is now "Son of Haskar", and a few other leftover names were cleaned up.
