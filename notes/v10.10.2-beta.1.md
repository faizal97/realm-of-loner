Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Fixes**
- Buying or taking an heirloom works again. Your bags are checked for room before any Mentor Marks are taken, so a full bag never costs you anything.
- If an heirloom purchase failed for you, the heirloom is already yours: take a copy now and it costs nothing.
- "Report a bug" in Settings now opens a short form with your details already filled in.
