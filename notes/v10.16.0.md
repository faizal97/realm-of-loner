**Highlights**
- Crafting is easier to find: from level 5 the game points you to the nearest profession trainer.
- **The Tidecrown Citadel** now opens after a **Veshmira's Lair** clear, and shows the quests that open it while it's locked.
- Heirlooms now sit a little under quest gear, so quest rewards are upgrades again.

**Changes**
- Clearing **Veshmira's Lair** (Normal or Hard) now lifts the storm over the isle, as well as handing in **The Brood Mother**.
- The Android app is now about 15 MB smaller.

**Balance**
- Heirlooms: stats at your level about 130% → 90% of a quest green's (**Veteran's Hammer** at 60: 44 → 31 stat points). They still give +5% experience each.
  *Dev note: a level-60 heirloom was as strong as raid loot, so nothing else was worth wearing in its slot. Heirlooms are for levelling fast, not for power.*
- **The Snuffed Road** (Hard): the three wisp waves now have 70% of their health and damage.
  *Dev note: tanks were going down in one or two hits while the wisps were up. The waves stay, they just hit less hard.*

**Interface**
- Added a **Sort** button to Bags: gear first, junk last, stacks merged.
- Added a one-time **Professions!** card from level 5 that names the nearest trainer, with **Show the way**.
- Gathering nodes now show before you learn the skill, marked **Needs Mining** or **Needs Herbalism**.
- The zone map now marks profession trainers with an anvil.
- The Professions screen now names the nearest trainer when you have no profession yet.
- **The Tidecrown Citadel**'s briefing now lists the quests that lift the storm, with the way to the next one.
- The Group Finder now lists the activities your quests need under **For your quests**, tagged **Your quest**.
- Gear power now says what 100% is: **Power: 76% of newest raid gear**.
- The **Potion** button now shows the potion a tap drinks.
- A place you can't reach now says which enemy town is on the road.
- Accepting a bounty now says it went into your Quest Log.
- A long fight or place name at the top of the screen now wraps to two lines; tap it to read it whole.
- The festival lantern no longer covers an open screen's **Back** and close buttons.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed the **Deserter** countdown only updating when the screen redrew.
- Fixed a player who left a run staying in the party list until the next pull.
