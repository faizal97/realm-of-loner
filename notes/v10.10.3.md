Quicker trips and fewer surprises: travel within a zone is about twice as fast, your bounties live in the Quest Log, your bags warn you before they fill up, and the Android app draws the game more smoothly.

**Faster travel nearby**
- Travel between two places in the same zone is about twice as fast: a typical hop takes about 9 seconds instead of 18, and about 5 with a mount.
- Travel into another zone, boats and other long routes keep their times, so far journeys still feel far, and the mount still speeds up every trip over land.

**Bounties in the Quest Log**
- The Quest Log (the **Quests** tab at the bottom) now has a **Bounties** section under your quests. Each bounty shows its progress and where it came from, or a **Ready** mark when it's done.
- Tap a bounty to see where its target spawns (nearest first), which board to hand it in at, when it runs out and what it pays. When you're standing at that board, **Hand in** is right there.
- The log's header counts both, for example "3/20 quests · 2/6 bounties".

**Bags**
- The Bags tab warns you before your bags are full: an amber number when 3 or fewer slots are free, and a red "Full" at 0. The Bags header and Bags in the run menu show it too.

**Android: smoother drawing**
- The app uses a smoother way of drawing the game.
- If it feels worse on your phone, you can switch back to the old mode: tap the version on the title screen eight times quickly. The change applies the next time you open the app.
- Tap the version five times quickly to show or hide a small frame-rate counter in the corner. One tap still opens What's new.

**Fixes**
- War Mode alerts in chat name the Accord and the Krugar.
- The Daily Roulette resets at your own midnight, as its message says. A run you already did today still counts until it resets.
- Two pop-up messages at once no longer cover each other: they stack, the newest below, at most three at a time, and the same message isn't shown twice.

**If you play on Android**, this update also brings the fixes the browser got earlier:
- Buying or taking an heirloom works again, and your bags are checked for room before any Mentor Marks are taken. If an heirloom purchase failed for you, the heirloom is already yours: take a copy now and it costs nothing.
- "Report a bug" in Settings opens a short form with your details already filled in.
