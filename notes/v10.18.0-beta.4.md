Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Stacks now hold 20, in the bags and the bank, and full bags waste nothing.
- Moving your device's clock no longer breaks timers, weekly goals, lockouts or festival dailies.
- Over a hundred fixes to the shared realm, chat requests, guilds, battlegrounds and group runs.

**Changes**
- Stacks now hold 20 in the bags and the bank. Older bigger stacks split into 20s when you log in.
- Gathering with full bags now keeps what fits and says what was left behind.
- Fishing, crafting and swaps now work with full bags when a stack has room or the materials free a slot.
- Leaving a battleground before the end now gives **Deserter** and counts as played.
- A battleground now starts everyone at full health and ends your trip.
- Closing the app mid-duel now counts as a loss, wager included. Mid-brawl, it counts the round as lost.
- A **Help Wanted** stuck at a raid's last boss no longer counts as a full raid clear.
- **Help Wanted** no longer sends you into dungeons the storm still hides. Neither does **Dungeon Roulette**.
- A group-finder pop now expires after 90 seconds, and a dropped queue tells you.
- A **Help Wanted** poster now leads the run and becomes a friend afterwards.
- A friend's run invite is now always for a dungeon that fits both your levels.
- Guild invites and recruiting posts now come from someone online, in a guild that takes your level.
- A Longnight item kept after the festival now works again next Longnight.
- A story scene no longer counts against par, unless a fight goes on under it.

**Interface**
- Request buttons in chat now say why they're greyed: in the group finder, party full, bags full, they're gone, **Deserter**.
- Selling or auctioning an upgraded item now warns that the upgrades are lost.
- The auction house sell dialog now shows **Usual price**, **Deposit** and **House cut** as rows.
- Counts now read 3/10 everywhere.
- Map place names no longer overlap each other or run off the edge.
- Enemies now sit right under the party during a pull, above the action bar.
- **Ready** and **Leave** now stay in view while the group rests, and the battleground's **Go** stays in view too.
- **Welcome back** now closes with a tap anywhere.
- Opening a setting now scrolls it into view.
- Renamed the trophy title to **the Trophy Hunter**, so it no longer shares its name with King Ghostpelt's.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed moving the device clock back leaving queues, trips, cooldowns, buffs, ghosts, casts, meals, auctions and chat a day late.
- Fixed moving the clock forward and back resetting weekly lockouts, festival dailies, bounties and the Trials month.
- Fixed moving the clock forward and back paying the weekly guild and Trials goals twice.
- Fixed leaving a guild and rejoining in the same week re-rolling or paying its weekly goal again.
- Fixed a save code ignoring the time since it was made.
- Fixed loading after a long time away freezing the game.
- Fixed a battleground or brawl left by closing the app staying stuck.
- Fixed a creature you were fighting when the app closed staying in its fight.
- Fixed Longnight items being deleted from the bags on every load, and rewards still owed being lost when it ends.
- Fixed festival items never stacking, and festival items in the bank outlasting the festival.
- Fixed an armour kit being lost on the next upgrade step.
- Fixed an heirloom kept in the bank keeping the stats of the level it went in at.
- Fixed a reward sent to the bank also saying the bags were full, and not joining a stack there.
- Fixed a gift being lost if you didn't tap **Thank them** in time.
- Fixed a party forming while queued, in a battleground or in a duel, and duels starting inside PvP.
- Fixed a guild application being answered after you'd joined another guild, or lost on reload.
- Fixed two **Help** tasks in different places breaking each other up.
- Fixed a guild night you signed up for pulling you into its run hours later.
- Fixed a finished battleground or brawl staying on screen over your next run.
- Fixed declining a pop using up a Legend's visit.
- Fixed a replacement joining a Trial fighting below level 60.
- Fixed a teammate's gear changing between the world and the group finder.
- Fixed party buff timers overlapping on the party frames.
- Fixed the **Dungeons** tab being cut off in the group finder.
- Fixed the level-up banner promising mana to classes without it.
- Fixed reaction names being written twice in ability and talent text.
