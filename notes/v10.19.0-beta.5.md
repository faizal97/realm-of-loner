Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

This build brings the beta up to date with the latest live patch (v10.18.3).

**Highlights**
- Added **Lock**: a locked piece of gear is never sold, thrown away or auctioned.
- New music for all three raids: **Veshmira's Lair**, **The Magma Throne** and **The Tidecrown Citadel**.
- The **Auction House** now always sells the basic materials your crafts need at your skill.

**New Content**
- Added **Lock** and **Unlock** to every piece of gear, in your bags and on your character. Locked items show a padlock.
- Added **Lock worn gear** under **Hero → Equipment**: one tap locks everything you're wearing.
- Added new raid music: **Harpsichord Counting** in **Veshmira's Lair**, **Forge of Ender** in **The Magma Throne** and **The Descent of Aelindra** in **The Tidecrown Citadel**.
- Added **This week** banners for 14–20 November: **The Notes Come Due**, a new set of **Featured dungeons**, the **Account bank** and **The Faire** coming.

**Changes**
- The **Auction House** now always lists the basic materials your crafts use at your skill, never below their value.
- At level 60, every town's **Bounty Board** now offers its own zone's monsters, at level-60 pay.
- **Torn debt notes** now trade for **Mentor Marks** only at level 60, 10 notes for 10 Marks. Any level still collects them for **the Debt-Breaker**.
  *Dev note: a low-level alt beating debt collectors made more Marks an hour than Trials. Marks are for level 60.*
- Selling several items now asks first when any of them is green, the same rule as selling one.
- **The Daily Board** and **The Weekly Board** now turn at your local midnight and your own Monday.
- Items in the **Account bank** now count as owned: a Trial find kept there no longer drops again.

**Interface**
- The death recap now shows your gear's condition and the repair cost, or how many pieces are broken.
- **Hero** now shows a badge when your gear is at 50% or less, red when something is broken.
- **Torn debt notes** now show your progress toward **the Debt-Breaker** (n/40) in the log, at the clerk and on **Hero**.
- At level 60, a one-time tip points at **This week**, and the **Quartermaster**'s note lists every way to earn **Mentor Marks**.
- **Hero → Journey**'s dungeon, Roulette, world boss, featured raid and Trials rows now open the **Groups** tab.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed **Sort** not showing when your bags held a single stack over 20, so it couldn't be split.
- Fixed **Sort** needing several presses to merge old stacks over 20.
- Fixed a craft losing its materials when the product didn't fit your bags. They now go to the bank.
- Fixed the gear-condition chip covering the talent-point dot on the **Hero** tab.
- Fixed a fight's tip card (**Red bar!**, **New reaction!**) covering the **You died** screen.
- Fixed chat buy, swap and guild-material requests saying "You have 0/5" after you stored materials in the bank.
- Fixed a guild invitation arriving when you were already in a guild.
- Fixed guildmates asking where places above their level are, and congratulating runs they couldn't do.
- Fixed "gn guys" from guildmates who then stayed online, and ding lines without a real level-up.
- Fixed yesterday's bounty and last week's guild goal showing as done until 07:00 for players east of UTC.
