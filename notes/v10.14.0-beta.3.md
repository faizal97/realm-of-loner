Test build, browser only: play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- New **Mail**: a letter for every auction you sell, and expired auctions wait in your mail instead of your bags.
- The **Bard**'s instruments are now one button that switches between the ones you know.

**New Content**
- Added **Mail**, opened from the envelope beside your gold; a dot shows unread letters.
- Added a letter for each auction sold: the item, the buyer and the gold you got (the gold still arrives at once).
- Added a letter for each expired auction, holding the item; take it at a mailbox in any town.

**Changes**
- A sold auction now shows a notice: "Sold: Iron Helm, 2g 40s".
- The **Bard**'s **Lute**, **Drum** and **Horn** are now one button, shown only outside a fight; tap it for your next instrument.

**Interface**
- The **Drum** and **Horn** have their own icons.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed the action bar gaining empty slots in a fight, for every class.
- Fixed **Paws for Proof** getting stuck in the Quest Log for some Accord characters.

**Known Issues**
- The **Bard** is not finished: its talents, art and how-to-play guide come in later betas, and its numbers will change.
