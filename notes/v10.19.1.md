**Changes**
- Gear power now shows as **Item Power**, a plain number where **Tidecrown Citadel** epics are 100: "87% of newest raid gear" is now **Item Power 87**. **Hero → Character** shows your average **Item Power**.
- Dungeon and raid briefings at level 60 now show the **Item Power** recommended there (what a careful group finder player wears), beside yours.
- Party rows and player cards now show each player's **Item Power**, so you can see who is well geared before the pull.
- Level-60 green gear now has an **Item Power** too, and your average counts every piece you wear.

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed **This week** saying **The Notes Come Due** arrives tomorrow when it's already on. The pre-patch and **Account bank** banners now show from today.
- Fixed raid members walking off the edge of the screen when several of them used **Step Back**.
- Fixed **This week** banners needing two taps, where the second tap could open the banner above instead.
- Fixed the group finder putting level-59 players in level-60 raids and dungeons, where they fought as level 59.
