Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Highlights**
- Gold and Mentor Marks have far more to buy at 60: fast mounts, bank tabs, raid set colours and 7 Quartermaster mounts.
- New: **Reforge** an item's effect or stats, and **Renown**, an account rank that never runs out.
- Gear now wears down when you die, and repairs cost gold.

**New Content**
- Added **Expert Riding** at level 60 (300g): with a swift mount, roads are 70% faster instead of 40%.
- Added a swift version of each faction's mounts, such as the **Swift Palomino** and **Swift Grey Wolf** (75g each).
- Added 7 **Mentor Quartermaster** mounts for Mentor Marks (500–900), any faction, all swift: the **Mentor's Warhorse**, **Ironbound Ram**, **Duskmane Wolf**, **Goldcrest Raptor**, **Ashen Dustback**, **Moonlit Shadowcat** and **Gilded Clockwork Trike**.
- Added every raid set look in 2 **Quartermaster** colours for the wardrobe (60 Marks an armour look, 70 a weapon look).
- Added 4 armour heirlooms: **Veteran's Jerkin**, **Legguards**, **Handwraps** and **Boots** (60 Marks each).
- Added 3 bank tabs of 12 slots each (50g, 100g, 200g).
- Added **Reforge** for level-57+ blue and purple gear: reroll its effect or its stats for Mentor Marks, then Keep or Revert.
- Added **Renown**: an account rank bought with Marks and gold, with a dye each rank, the title **the Renowned** every 5 and a mount tint every 10.
- Added **Crystal Vial** and **Fine Spice**, vendor reagents for level-57+ flasks and **Well Fed** meals (1g 50s each).

**Changes**
- Gear now loses 10% durability each time you die (a wipe counts). At 0% it is broken and gives no stats until repaired.
- Repair at any vendor with **Repair all**, or with a **Repair kit** between pulls in a dungeon (25% more).
- Reforge stats only rolls stats your class uses.
- The Mentor Quartermaster now has 3 items at 20% off each week.

**Balance**
- Raids: 15 Mentor Marks on each raid's first clear of the week, then 5 a clear.
  *Dev note: repeating Veshmira's Lair paid more Marks an hour than anything else; Trials and battlegrounds pay better now.*
- Level-57+ flasks and Well Fed meals now need a reagent: keeping both up costs about 3.75g an hour.
  *Dev note: gold piled up at 60 with nothing to spend it on. Levelling crafts take no reagent.*

**Known Issues**
- Quartermaster colour icons show the plain item, not the colour, so check a look in the wardrobe after buying it.
- **Repair all** is at the bottom of the vendor list.
