Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

This build brings the beta up to date with today's live update (v10.18.2).

**Highlights**
- Added **This week**: a button on every screen that shows what's on in the realm and what to do next.
- Added **Featured dungeons**: one old dungeon for every level range each week, with ×1.5 speed and no-wipe rewards.

**New Content**
- Added **This week**: banners for what's on now, what's new and what's coming. Tap a banner for what it is and its steps, which tick off as you play, each with **Go** to the right screen.
- **This week** opens by itself the first time you come in after something new is added. It's also in **Hero → Journey**, and **Settings → This week button** hides the button.
- During a festival, the button is the festival's lantern, as before.
- Added **Featured dungeons** to **This week**: each week, one old dungeon for every level range. Speed and no-wipe rewards there pay ×1.5, and your first clear of one each week pays gold and a green item (15 Mentor Marks at level 60). They're marked in the group finder.
- The **featured raid** now has its own banner in **This week** at level 60.
- **This week** now shows your progress at the top, **Today** and **This week**: tap one to see what's left, each with a **Go**, and what's done.
- Added **The Weekly Board** and **The Daily Board** to **This week** from level 10: this week's and today's bounties, Roulette, Brawl chest, guild goal, world boss and Trials, each ticking itself off.
